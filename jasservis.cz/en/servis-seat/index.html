SEAT servicing

  • Home
  • Vehicle servicing
  • SEAT servicing

We specialize in diagnostics, maintenance and repairs of SEAT vehicles manufactured from 2010 onwards. We determine the specific scope of work according to the model, engine, equipment and technical condition of the vehicle.

For diagnosis, we use the original ODIS system, professional measuring equipment and specialised service equipment. We assess the results of the diagnostics together with the vehicle inspection and proceed according to the manufacturer's technical information during the repair.

We adapt regular service inspections, changes of oils, filters and other vehicle fluids to the service plan of a particular car. We will agree the scope of the necessary repair and the expected price with the customer before starting the work.

J&S SERVIS has been operating in Statenice – Černý Vůl since 1994. As an independent car service, we continue our long-standing focus on Volkswagen Group technology when servicing the SEAT brand.

SEAT vehicle servicing and maintenance

We carry out regular service inspections, replacement of engine and transmission oils, filters, timing belts and chains, repairs of brake systems, chassis, steering and air conditioning.

We service, for example, SEAT Ibiza, Leon, Arona, Ateca, Tarraco and other SEAT models.

Diagnostics and repairs of engines and gearboxes

We specialize in the diagnosis and repair of TSI petrol engines and TDI diesel engines, including turbocharging, injection, cooling and emissions systems. We also provide diagnostics, maintenance and repairs of DSG automatic transmissions according to their construction and technical condition.

Original ODIS diagnostics

We use ODIS, professional measuring equipment and dedicated workshop tools. We carry out functional tests, assess measured values and perform basic settings and adaptations within the vehicle's technical capabilities.

Hybrid and electric vehicles

We confirm the scope of maintenance and diagnostics in advance for the specific vehicle. High-voltage traction battery repairs and work on high-voltage components are not included in this offer.
