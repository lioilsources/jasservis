Audi servicing

  • Home
  • Vehicle servicing
  • Audi servicing

We specialize in diagnostics, maintenance and repairs of Audi vehicles manufactured from 2010 onwards. The specific scope of service work depends on the model, engine variant, equipment and workshop’s technical capabilities.

When searching for defects, we use original ODIS diagnostics, professional measuring equipment and manufacturer's service procedures. We propose a repair according to the results of measurements and checking the actual condition of the vehicle, not just according to the error code.

We carry out regular service inspections and replacement of vehicle fluids according to the relevant service plan. We consult the scope of the repair and the estimated price with the customer before starting the work.

J&S SERVIS has been operating in Statenice – Černý Vůl since 1994. As an independent car service, we apply many years of experience with Volkswagen Group technology also when servicing Audi vehicles.

  • Expert Audi diagnostics and repairs
  • Original ODIS diagnostics
  • Experience with VW Group technology since 1994

Audi engine diagnostics and repairs

We carry out diagnostics and repairs of TFSI and TSI petrol engines and TDI diesel engines. We focus on finding and eliminating faults in timing drives, turbocharging, injection and fuel systems, cooling and emission systems.

Our services include diagnosis of irregular engine operation, loss of power, excessive consumption of vehicle fluids and other mechanical or electronic faults.

S tronic and tiptronic automatic transmissions

We provide diagnostics, service and repairs of Audi automatic transmissions according to the specific design and type of transmission.

We change transmission oils and filters, check operating parameters and carry out functional tests, prescribed basic settings and selected mechanical repairs.

DSG and S tronic servicing

Audi diagnostics – ODIS

We use the ODIS diagnostic system, professional measuring equipment and specialised service equipment.

We perform:

  • Diagnostics of control units and evaluation of fault memories.
  • Checking measured values and operating parameters.
  • Functional tests of actuators and electronic systems.
  • Prescribed basic settings and adaptations.
  • Diagnostics and search for the causes of more complex technical faults.

The range of individual diagnostic functions depends on the specific model, equipment and technical conditions of the vehicle.

Regular Audi servicing and maintenance

We provide regular service inspections, replacement of engine and transmission oils, filters and vehicle fluids, service of brake systems, chassis, steering and other mechanical and electronic repairs.

We carry out service and repair work according to the relevant service procedures of the manufacturer.

Hybrid and electric vehicles

We confirm the scope of maintenance and diagnostics in advance for the specific vehicle. High-voltage traction battery repairs and work on high-voltage components are not included in this offer.
