J&S servis – independent specialised car service for Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT and CUPRA vehicles since 1994.

We carry out professional replacements of timing belts and chains in accordance with the service procedures of the manufacturer. During repairs, we use the prescribed arresting, assembly and measuring devices.

  • Electronic valve timing measurement with VAS 611 007
  • Timing belt and chain replacement to manufacturer procedures
  • Specializing in VW Group since 1994

Price

  • TSI timing drives: from 6 900 Kč including VAT – only work on selected engines. Parts and material separately, range by VIN.
  • TDI timing belts: from 4 100 Kč including VAT – only work on selected engines. Parts and material separately, range by VIN.

Precision electronic valve timing measurement and adjustment

We have a complete electronic measuring system VAS 611 007 including appropriate adapter sets. For selected engines, we use it to accurately measure and set the camshaft timing according to the manufacturer's service procedures.

Price list of work including VAT:

  • Selected engines 1.0, 1.2, 1.4 and 1.5 TSI - from 6 900 Kč
  • Selected engines 1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.9 and 2.0 TDI - from 4 100 Kč
  • Other engines - individual calculation.

The prices shown cover labour for timing belt or chain replacement within the relevant scope only. Spare parts, vehicle fluids and consumables are charged separately.

We verify the relevant price variant, the service procedure and the scope of the repair according to the VIN of the vehicle. Before starting the work, we will present the customer with a total price calculation.
