CUPRA servicing

  • Home
  • Vehicle servicing
  • CUPRA servicing

We specialize in diagnostics, maintenance and repairs of CUPRA vehicles. We determine the scope of work according to the specific model, drive, transmission, equipment and technical condition of the vehicle.

When searching for the causes of faults, we use original ODIS diagnostics, professional measuring equipment and manufacturer's service information. We connect the results of the diagnostics with a check of the actual condition of the vehicle and supplement it with targeted tests as needed.

We carry out regular maintenance according to the service plan of a specific car. We choose spare parts and vehicle fluids according to the prescribed specifications. We agree the scope of work and the expected price with the customer before starting the repair and document the performed actions.

J&S SERVIS has been operating in Statenice – Černý Vůl since 1994. We also apply our long-term focus on the technology of the Volkswagen Group concern when servicing CUPRA vehicles.

  • Original diagnostics ODIS.
  • Maintenance according to the manufacturer's service plan.
  • Experience with VW Group technology since 1994.

Expert CUPRA diagnostics and servicing

For CUPRA vehicles, diagnostics and maintenance reflect the engine, transmission, equipment and manufacturer procedures. We use ODIS, operating data measurements and functional tests to identify fault causes. A fault memory entry alone is not sufficient reason to replace a component.

Engines, transmissions and chassis

We provide service and diagnostics of TSI petrol engines, selected hybrid drives and DSG transmissions to the extent appropriate to the vehicle's design. We also specialize in brake, chassis and electronic systems. We determine service intervals, specifications of vehicle fluids and any basic settings according to the specific design of the vehicle.

Electrified CUPRA models

For selected plug-in hybrid and electric vehicles, we provide routine maintenance and diagnostics within the workshop's technical capabilities and available authorisations. High-voltage battery repairs and work on high-voltage components are not included in this offer.
