Engine carbon cleaning

  • Home
  • Engine carbon cleaning

J&S SERVIS – independent specialised car service for Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT and CUPRA vehicles since 1994.

Engine decarbonization – targeted chemical and mechanical cleaning according to the design and technical condition of the engine.

For petrol and diesel engines, we deal with carbon and other operating deposits in the oil, fuel and intake system. We choose the appropriate procedure according to the specific engine, symptoms and inspection results. Not every cleaning method is suitable for every vehicle.

  • Chemical cleaning of the oil, fuel and intake or injection system by BG Products
  • Intensive cleaning of the oil system with BG Dynamic Engine Cleaner
  • Mechanical removal of carbon deposits from intake valves and ports with cleaning granules with simultaneous suction using VW AG equipment (VAS 294 011/1A)

How decarbonisation works

Chemical cleaning with BG Products: depending on the chosen procedure and suitability for the given engine, we clean the oil, fuel, injection or intake system. We use BG Dynamic Engine Cleaner for intensive cleaning of the oil system. The individual procedures are not interchangeable and we choose them according to the specific condition of the vehicle.

We mechanically remove carbon deposits from intake valves and ports using cleaning granules with simultaneous extraction and VW AG equipment (VAS 294 011/1A). This generally requires the intake manifold to be removed.

For mechanical granule cleaning, we prepare an individual estimate based on labour, granule consumption, intake manifold removal and refitting, and materials. The exact granule consumption is known only after cleaning.

What is important to know: decarbonization is a targeted service intervention, not a universal engine repair. It will not eliminate mechanical wear or all causes of increased oil consumption, erratic running or loss of power. Before starting, we will recommend a suitable procedure and discuss the scope and estimated price with you in advance.

Before booking

We explain the specific BG procedure, the required fluids and filters and the expected costs in advance. A general oil-system cleaning price does not automatically cover the complete intensive-cleaning process. We assess suitability according to the engine type and condition.
