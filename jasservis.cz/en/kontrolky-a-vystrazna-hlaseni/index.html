WARNING LIGHTS IN VW GROUP VEHICLES

  • Home

  • Warning lights in VW GROUP vehicles

Has a warning light come on? Its meaning depends on the symbol, colour, whether it is steady or flashing, and any display message. Some indicators confirm an active function; others signal a restriction or a fault requiring immediate action.

We are preparing an illustrated overview for Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT, CUPRA and Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles. Only the entries documented below are displayed. Symbols and instructions can vary with model, model year, equipment and powertrain.

Always follow the vehicle message and the owner’s manual for your vehicle. A lamp alone does not identify a failed part or replace professional diagnosis of the cause.

Does the vehicle display STOP or tell you not to continue driving?

Stop safely and follow the vehicle’s instructions. This web overview must never be a reason to ignore a warning. In immediate danger, first ensure the occupants’ safety.
