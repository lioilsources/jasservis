Practical guidance for responding to vehicle messages and checking basic operating information. Always verify a specific value for the exact model, model year, equipment and powertrain.

Warning lights and driver messages

Select your vehicle brand. You will see an overview of warning lights and symbols, their meaning and the recommended action. Always assess the meaning together with the message on the display and the owner’s manual for your vehicle.

Illustrated overview of warning lights and icons

The warning-light graphic may differ for a specific model or equipment version. Its exact meaning and function must be verified using the vehicle VIN, the displayed message and the corresponding owner’s manual.

Glow plug lamp – preheating before starting

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On a diesel engine it lights during preheating. This normal state before starting is not itself a fault.

Recommended action

Wait for the lamp to go out before starting, as instructed in the vehicle’s manual.

Key indication

Only the listed versions and equipment are covered. Follow the exact message and your vehicle’s manual.

Diesel preheating and management

Yellow / amber · Information / warning depending on state · As described in the source

Meaning

Recommended action

Follow the message and arrange inspection. The symbol does not automatically identify defective glow plugs.

Key indication

Brief illumination before starting; illumination or flashing while running.

Engine oil pressure

Red · Warning · As described in the source

Meaning

The warning indicates insufficient engine oil pressure. A correct oil level alone does not confirm adequate lubrication pressure.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. Do not leave it idling. Further steps, including any restart, must follow the specific vehicle manual; if unsure, obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Do not confuse this with a yellow oil level warning. The source describes a conditional procedure after topping up; this is not general permission to restart.

Combustion engine cooling system

Red · Warning · As described in the source

Meaning

May indicate overheating, an incorrect coolant level or a cooling system fault.

Recommended action

Stop safely, switch off the engine and follow the vehicle message. Check coolant as instructed only after safe cooling. Do not open a hot expansion tank.

Key indication

Visible coolant in the reservoir does not by itself resolve the warning. This entry does not cover electric-drive cooling.

Engine management and emissions – MIL

Yellow / amber · Warning · As described in the source

Meaning

The control system detected an engine management or emissions fault. The lamp alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Distinguish steady illumination from flashing. On relevant versions, flashing can indicate misfiring. If the engine shakes, vibrates or loses power markedly, stop safely and contact professional assistance. Otherwise follow the message and vehicle manual.

Key indication

Clearing a fault code does not remove the cause. This entry does not give universal permission to continue driving.

ID.4 – battery depleted, drive shutdown imminent

Red · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

While driving, a red turtle with the corresponding message indicates a very low high-voltage battery charge and imminent vehicle immobilisation. Power is reduced.

Recommended action

Pull over and park safely as traffic permits; use a charging station only if it can be reached safely. Follow the full message and manual. Do not infer remaining range from the symbol.

Key indication

ID.4 11/2021 distinguishes a red turtle while driving with a depleted battery from a red turtle before starting when the battery is too cold. This entry concerns depletion while driving; the exact message is decisive.

ID.4 – battery too cold, driving not possible

Meaning

Key indication

This entry applies to the stated ID.4 edition. It is not electric-drive overheating or a universal meaning of a red turtle on every EV.
