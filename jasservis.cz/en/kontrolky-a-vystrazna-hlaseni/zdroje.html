Work in progress – not a complete catalogue.

35 distinct pictogram shapes; 47 graphic colour variants; 88 semantic records. Repeated brand or model assignments are not additional unique warning lights.

MY means the model year assigned in the manufacturer’s catalogue, not a confirmed production year for an individual vehicle. Always compare the symbol with your vehicle. Graphics are standardised schematic depictions, not photographs of instrument clusters.

Before publication, a technician must review the content, graphic assignments and model-specific differences. SSPs provide supplementary technical context; they do not replace owner’s manuals. Czech documents and text within photographs are not presented as translated.

Explaining the change from 73 to 86

All 73 original semantic records were retained, including their texts and model assignments. Thirteen new records were added: seven for Škoda and six for commercial vehicles. None were merged, replaced or removed.

Škoda: 43 brand entries = 36 assignments of existing records + 7 new records. Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles: 24 brand entries = 18 assignments of existing records + 6 new records. Brand-card count rose from 107 to 174; this does not mean 67 new unique meanings.

This reconciliation specifically compares versions db2c200 and 279c4c9. Subsequent expansions are reported separately.
