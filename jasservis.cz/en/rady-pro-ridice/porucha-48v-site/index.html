A 48 V electrical system fault in a mild hybrid

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle version. Selecting a brand alone does not identify the exact vehicle.

What an electrical system warning means and how its cause is investigated

Which vehicles this advice applies to

Vehicles with a 48 V mild hybrid system. The meaning of a warning and the repair options depend on the model, generation and equipment.

What to do now

How to respond to a warning

Situation Recommended next step
An instruction to stop safely Stop safely and follow the message and the vehicle's handbook. Seek professional assistance to determine the next steps.
An instruction not to start driving Do not enter traffic. Follow the specific handbook and the instruction on the display.
Several warnings at the same time After stopping safely, record their wording and order, along with any problems starting or using other functions.
The warning later disappears Tell the workshop about this too. A temporary return to normal operation does not confirm that the cause has been removed.

Why an inspection should not be delayed

If charging fails, continued battery discharge may cause further restrictions, potentially leading to a loss of vehicle power. A running engine does not, on its own, confirm that it is safe to continue.

Do not work on the 48 V battery, its connections or wiring yourself. The next steps and any return to operation require professional inspection for the specific system.

What the symptom may mean

An electrical system fault message, a battery symbol or restricted mild hybrid functions need to be assessed for the specific vehicle. The same outward symptom may be related to different parts of the electrical system.

The exact wording of the message determines the next steps. If the vehicle tells you to stop or not to start driving, follow that instruction. The colour of the warning light alone is not enough to decide whether you can continue.

Why both electrical systems are assessed

A vehicle with a 48 V mild hybrid system generally also has a 12 V vehicle electrical system. A voltage converter is one of the components that enables them to work together. The system includes batteries, a starter-generator, the associated wiring and the control of their operation.

When investigating the cause, the power supply therefore needs to be checked in context. Checking a single battery or one instantaneous voltage reading may not reflect the actual condition of the complete system.

The starter-generator may not be the original cause

The starter-generator contributes to starting the engine and generating electrical energy. Its operation may also be restricted as a result of another electrical or communication fault. Charging may then fail to work correctly even though the original cause lies elsewhere.

A fault in the starter-generator itself is also possible. A decision to replace it should be based on a diagnostic test and the verified condition of the system, not merely on the component's name appearing in a fault message.

Mild hybrid systems differ in design

The designation 48 V does not mean that all vehicles have the same layout. Some newer systems use an additional electric drive alongside the starter-generator. Experience with one design may therefore not apply to another.

What to tell the workshop

For a quicker discussion, have the VIN, current mileage and a brief description of the requested work or fault ready. We confirm the appointment and scope of work by agreement.

How the cause is checked

What a professional inspection should check

Depending on the vehicle's design, checks cover the condition of both batteries, the power supply, wiring and connections, the converter and communication between components. It is important to distinguish the initial fault from further messages that arose as a result.

Generator output is controlled according to the vehicle's needs. One reading taken while the engine is running may therefore be insufficient to confirm or rule out a fault. Once the cause has been removed, the restoration of the relevant functions needs to be verified.
