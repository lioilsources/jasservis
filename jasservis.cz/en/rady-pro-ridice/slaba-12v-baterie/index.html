A weak 12 V battery

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • A weak 12 V battery

←Back to driver advice

Slower starting, a low-charge warning or restricted convenience functions can relate to the vehicle's power supply. Deciding whether to replace the battery requires more than one voltage reading.

Which vehicles this advice applies to

Volkswagen Group vehicles with a 12 V electrical system, including hybrids and electric vehicles. Battery specification, charging and any replacement registration are vehicle-specific.

What to do now

Record the exact message and when it appears. If the vehicle says that its drive system cannot be restarted after shutdown, do not repeatedly switch it off and on as a troubleshooting attempt. Follow the message and handbook and agree the next step with a workshop or roadside assistance.

Only charge or jump-start using the method and connection points approved for your vehicle. In an electric car, this does not charge the traction battery.

Discharge and wear need different solutions

A low state of charge may relate to short trips, extended parking or electrical consumption while parked. Alternatively, the battery may have lost performance, or charging, monitoring or electrical connections may be at fault.

State of charge must therefore be distinguished from battery condition. Charging alone may not solve repeated discharge. An unavailable start-stop function does not by itself prove battery failure.

Why electric vehicles are affected too

The traction battery stores energy for driving. The vehicle also uses a low-voltage electrical system for control and other functions. A fault there can prevent drive activation even when the traction battery display shows sufficient charge.

A 12 V warning, a 48 V system fault and a high-voltage drive fault must be distinguished. Their causes and repair procedures differ.

Checks and replacement

Relevant information includes previous discharges, battery age and type, test results and charging performance.

AGM, EFB and the correct replacement

AGM and EFB are different lead-acid battery technologies. Replacement depends on vehicle specifications, technology, capacity, starting current and construction; matching dimensions or Ah alone are not sufficient. Disabling start-stop does not permit an unsuitable conventional battery. Any change of technology must be allowed for the specific vehicle.

A replacement battery must meet the vehicle's requirements. Where the energy management system requires the replacement to be registered, this is part of completing the job correctly. Data and procedures cannot simply be copied from another model.

The battery keeps going flat overnight

Note how long after parking the problem returns, how long your recent journeys were and whether a dashcam or other accessory remains connected.

We first check the state of charge, the condition of the battery and whether the charging system works properly. If current draw while parked is suspected, we monitor it from switch-off until the electronic systems go to sleep. For an intermittent fault, a longer recording can also capture a system repeatedly waking up.
