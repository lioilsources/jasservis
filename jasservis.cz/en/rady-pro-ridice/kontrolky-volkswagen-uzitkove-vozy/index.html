Warning lights and driver messages
Engine, lubrication and cooling

EPC – petrol engine management

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The petrol engine management system has detected a fault. EPC does not identify a particular failed component.

Recommended action

Arrange engine inspection promptly. Brief illumination with the ignition on is a check; the lamp should go out after starting.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Emissions system – MIL steadily lit

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

An emissions system fault has been detected. The symbol alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Reduce load and arrange inspection as directed by the manual and message. If running is abnormal, other warnings appear or STOP is displayed, stop safely and seek assistance.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Glow plug lamp – preheating before starting

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On a diesel engine it lights during preheating. This normal state before starting is not itself a fault.

Recommended action

Wait for the lamp to go out before starting, as instructed in the vehicle’s manual.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Combustion engine cooling system

Red · Warning · Steady illumination

Meaning

May indicate overheating, an incorrect coolant level or a cooling system fault.

Recommended action

Stop safely, switch off the engine and follow the vehicle message. Check coolant as instructed only after safe cooling. Do not open a hot expansion tank.

Key indication

Visible coolant in the reservoir does not by itself resolve the warning. This entry does not cover electric-drive cooling.

Diesel particulate filter – DPF

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady illumination

Meaning

On relevant diesel versions it indicates accumulated soot and a need for filter regeneration.

Recommended action

Check regeneration driving conditions in your specific manual. If other warnings appear or engine operation is abnormal, do not follow a universal internet regeneration procedure.

Key indication

No single speed, duration or engine speed applies to all engines. Petrol GPF/OPF requires separate verification.

Engine oil pressure – flashing red warning

Red · Warning · Flashing

Meaning

A flashing red symbol with an oil-pressure fault message warns of inadequate lubrication. A correct oil level does not prove correct pressure.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. Do not leave it running. Any restart must explicitly follow the instructions for the specific vehicle; obtain professional assistance if uncertain.

Key indication

Follow the exact message and your vehicle’s manual. The general guide does not document every model variant.

Electromechanical brake servo not working

Red · Warning · Steady

Meaning

Brake assistance is not working. More pedal force is required and stopping distances increase.

Recommended action

Stop safely, applying greater brake pedal force as required. Do not continue driving; obtain professional assistance immediately.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Brake system

Red · Warning

Meaning

Depending on the version, the symbol may relate to an applied parking brake, low brake fluid or a brake system fault.

Recommended action

The exact symbol and message are decisive. If the warning remains after releasing the parking brake or the vehicle reports a brake fault, stop safely and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Topping up brake fluid does not repair the cause of its loss. Distinguish a fault warning from the normal parking brake indication.

ESC / TCS intervention

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Flashing

Meaning

Flashing indicates that stability or traction control is intervening. It does not by itself indicate a fault.

Recommended action

Adapt speed and driving style to available grip and traffic conditions. The system cannot overcome physical limits.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

ESC fault or system-induced deactivation

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

A lamp that stays on after the initial check may indicate a fault or system-induced deactivation. This differs from flashing during intervention.

Recommended action

Read the message and follow the cited manual. If the lamp remains on, arrange professional inspection; do not rely on stability control being available.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Brake servo – restricted operation, amber warning

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

With the documented amber warning, electromechanical braking assistance is restricted. Pedal pulsations may occur and stopping distance can increase.

Recommended action

Allow for greater brake-pedal effort and seek professional service. Other simultaneous messages, including STOP, take priority; amber is not universal assurance that continued travel is safe.

Key indication

Different from red servo failure with the instruction not to continue. In another message the same sign can refer to another brake component; exact text and cited edition are decisive.

ASR – traction control manually off (Caddy)

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On this Caddy, the steady amber skidding-car OFF sign confirms manual ASR deactivation. Not automatically a fault or complete deactivation of every stability function.

Recommended action

Keep ASR enabled in normal conditions. Follow the manual when switching it off for loose surfaces or freeing a stuck vehicle. Re-enable ASR once that situation has passed.

Key indication

Not the plain symbol flashing during ASR/ESC intervention. Control is in infotainment ESC settings; menu name alone does not establish that all ESC functions are off.

Driver assistance systems

ACC – take control and brake

Red · Warning · Steady

Meaning

Automatic ACC deceleration is insufficient in the current situation. The vehicle requests driver intervention. Do not confuse this red symbol with the green prompt during gear selection.

Recommended action

Take control of speed immediately and brake as necessary. Do not wait for ACC to resolve the danger by itself.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Take-over steering request

White · Information / warning depending on state · Steady

Meaning

The vehicle asks the driver to take over steering. This does not confirm that the system can handle the situation independently.

Recommended action

Safely take over steering, monitor traffic and follow the exact vehicle message. Be ready to brake.

Key indication

The indicator illuminates white and an accompanying prompt appears; the reviewed chapters do not state flashing. The red request has higher urgency. Association with Travel Assist, Emergency Assist or Lane Assist depends on equipment and the cited manual.

Take over steering immediately

Red · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The vehicle requests immediate driver steering control.

Recommended action

Safely take over steering immediately, monitor traffic and follow the exact vehicle message. Be ready to brake.

Key indication

The indicator illuminates red with an accompanying message; depending on the situation, a sound or steering-wheel vibration follows. The reviewed chapters do not state flashing. Association with Travel Assist, Emergency Assist or Lane Assist depends on equipment and the cited manual.
