Coolant level keeps falling

A repeatedly falling level requires investigation even if the engine is not overheating. Topping up does not repair a leak.

What to do now

For a red warning, STOP instruction, steam or overheating, stop safely and switch off the engine. Do not open a hot or pressurised reservoir. An empty reservoir must not be addressed simply by topping up and continuing to drive.

Information to have ready

VIN, how often and how much coolant is added, photographs of traces of coolant and details of the last cooling-system repair.

Arrange a vehicle check

The actual equipment and vehicle version determine the inspection scope and procedure.

Symptoms and inspection in detail

Loss without a puddle under the vehicle

A leak need not leave a visible puddle. It may depend on temperature and pressure or be concealed. Coolant loss alone does not establish a failed pump or cylinder-head gasket.

What should the inspection establish?

The initial level is checked under manufacturer-specified conditions, along with leaks and associated symptoms. Pressure-test methods and permissible values depend on the circuit. Correct filling and bleeding also form part of completing a repair.

Correct fluid and multiple circuits

The related coolant guide covers specifications and topping up. In a hybrid or electric vehicle, not every reservoir belongs to the combustion engine. Battery and electric-drive circuits require separate assessment; do not top up an unidentified or sealed reservoir.
