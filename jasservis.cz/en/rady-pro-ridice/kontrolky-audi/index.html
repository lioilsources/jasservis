Engine, lubrication and cooling

Emissions system – MIL steadily lit · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Emissions system – MIL steadily lit

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

An emissions system fault has been detected. The symbol alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Reduce load and arrange inspection as directed by the manual and message. If running is abnormal, other warnings appear or STOP is displayed, stop safely and seek assistance.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Audi emissions system – indicator lit or flashing

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady / flashing

Meaning

Steady or flashing illumination indicates an emissions-related fault that can worsen exhaust quality and damage the catalytic converter. Depending on engine and country, the indicator can also illuminate if the fuel cap is not properly closed. The symbol alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Check that the fuel cap is properly closed. If the indicator flashes or remains illuminated, drive immediately at reduced speed to a qualified workshop and have the fault corrected. Follow every accompanying message.

Key indication

Illumination during the ignition check does not by itself establish a fault. Whether the indicator goes out after the check, its subsequent behaviour and the accompanying message are decisive. The A8 manual gives one instruction for steady and flashing states, so they are not artificially split here.

Diesel particulate filter – DPF · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Diesel particulate filter – DPF

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

On relevant diesel versions it indicates accumulated soot and a need for filter regeneration.

Recommended action

Check regeneration driving conditions in your specific manual. If other warnings appear or engine operation is abnormal, do not follow a universal internet regeneration procedure.

Key indication

No single speed, duration or engine speed applies to all engines. Petrol GPF/OPF requires separate verification.

Petrol particulate filter – regeneration required

Yellow / amber · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

On the documented petrol implementation, the filter requires regeneration. The symbol alone does not identify a damaged filter.

Recommended action

First identify the exact engine and the manual procedure. Do not attempt a generic regeneration drive with other warnings or abnormal operation; arrange a professional check if the message persists.

Key indication

Q3 11/2024 specifies different procedures for different petrol engines. This is not a universal GPF/OPF procedure or a diesel DPF procedure.

Direct tyre-pressure monitoring – low pressure

Yellow / amber · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

In the specified direct system, a steady amber tyre with exclamation mark can indicate pressure below the stored reference. The accompanying message determines the exact meaning.

Recommended action

Check all tyres and correct pressure to the specified values at the next safe opportunity. Store correct reference values only after checking; stored values do not themselves prove compliance with the manufacturer’s specification.

Key indication

Only equipment with direct wheel pressure/temperature sensors. The same symbol with “Reifenpanne!” or “Radwechsel erkannt” has a different meaning. Do not transfer the procedure to indirect monitoring. In the direct system’s numerical display, green means approximately matching the stored reference, amber low pressure and red substantially low pressure. Dashes while stationary or learning are not measured pressure. Check tyre pressure at the appropriate temperature according to the manual; releasing air from hot tyres is not a general procedure.

Direct tyre-pressure monitoring – “Reifenpanne!” warning

Yellow / amber · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

The symbol together with the explicit puncture message indicates critically low pressure relative to the stored reference. Amber alone is not permission to continue.

Recommended action

Avoid unnecessary abrupt steering and braking. Stop safely at the next opportunity and inspect the tyres. Actual condition and the manual determine whether further travel is possible; do not merely reset the warning.

Key indication

Applies to the exact “Reifenpanne! Fahrzeug sicher abstellen. Bitte alle Reifen prüfen und im MMI speichern” message with the specified direct system. The symbol alone establishes neither damage extent nor a safe remaining range. In the direct system’s numerical display, green means approximately matching the stored reference, amber low pressure and red substantially low pressure. Dashes while stationary or learning are not measured pressure. Check tyre pressure at the appropriate temperature according to the manual; releasing air from hot tyres is not a general procedure.

Direct tyre-pressure monitoring – wheel change detected

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Symbol with message

Meaning

With the “Radwechsel erkannt” message, the same amber symbol can report changed sensor positions after a wheel swap or newly fitted sensors. Not automatically the same as critically low pressure.

Recommended action

First check all tyres and their specified pressures, then store correct reference values using the actual MMI procedure. The driver remains responsible for correct pressure; confirmation does not repair a tyre.

Key indication

Meaning is documented only with the exact message and direct sensors. The icon alone does not distinguish low pressure, critical loss and a wheel change. In the direct system’s numerical display, green means approximately matching the stored reference, amber low pressure and red substantially low pressure. Dashes while stationary or learning are not measured pressure. Check tyre pressure at the appropriate temperature according to the manual; releasing air from hot tyres is not a general procedure.
