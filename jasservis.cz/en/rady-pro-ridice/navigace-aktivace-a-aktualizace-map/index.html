Navigation will not start, or you need new maps? Navigation activation, map updates and online services are checked separately.

A NAV button alone does not confirm active navigation.

Navigation will not start

What to check when navigation will not start

Possible causes include an unactivated preparation, missing or incorrect medium, unsupported map version or a fault in the unit or medium.

State whether navigation worked before and whether the unit was replaced or modified.

At J&S SERVIS, we first check the unit, the role of the medium and the available official route. We then confirm whether a data update, new medium, activation document or fault assessment is required.

I need new maps

Choose the update method for your navigation unit. If you are unsure, start by checking available maps and the manufacturer’s instructions.

Where to get maps and what may be free

Where to check maps and free downloads

Where offered, add the vehicle by VIN on the manufacturer's portal and check available maps, installation instructions and licence conditions. Other offers use the navigation unit identification and region.

Free map-file downloads do not automatically include free activation, a new storage medium or workshop labour. If the infotainment was replaced or modified, check the unit actually installed as well as the VIN.

Maps on CD or DVD

Navigation CDs

Older systems store maps on a compatible navigation CD. TravelPilot DX is one example. Updating requires the correct map disc, rather than the SD-card process used by a modern navigation system.

Check the format, geographical coverage and actual age of the map data. A different-format CD is not a substitute merely because it is labelled navigation. A newly sold disc can still contain old maps.

Check the available legitimate release before ordering. Current road coverage and continuing manufacturer support cannot be assumed for old formats.

Navigation DVDs

Map DVDs belong to different navigation families from map CDs. Depending on the unit, a DVD supplies map data during use or installs it into internal storage.

Select the version for the exact unit and region. The relevant instructions determine whether the disc must remain in the drive and whether additional activation is required. Simply inserting a DVD may not replace installation or licence activation.

SD cards: original maps or an update medium?

Original map SD cards

This card is part of everyday navigation operation. The unit reads maps from it and the card must stay in the appropriate reader. Where an original or manufacturer-approved card is required, copying files onto an ordinary blank SD card does not replace it.

Different situations may apply:

  • The manufacturer permits map downloads and updates on the correct original card within the available entitlement.
  • A new release requires the correct original medium or another prescribed entitlement.
  • The original card is missing or damaged and needs a compatible replacement.

Check update entitlement before overwriting the original card. Back up the original files and follow the manufacturer instructions. Do not erase or format the card before checking these conditions.

SD cards used for installation or updating

An installation SD card has a different role from an operational map SD card. Its data is transferred into the navigation unit. Once installation finishes correctly, the card may no longer be needed for everyday navigation, according to the manufacturer instructions.

Some systems accept an ordinary card of the specified capacity and format; others require a purchased update medium. Entitlement to use new maps may be included or checked and activated separately.

“Update on SD card” alone does not establish whether a licence, original card or ordinary installation medium is needed. Installation and operational SD cards cannot be distinguished by appearance alone.

Updates through USB or online

Updating through USB

For supported systems, download the map package to a computer, extract it as instructed and prepare the specified USB medium. Use the correct data USB port in the vehicle; the presence of a USB socket alone does not determine the procedure.

Capacity, formatting and file structure follow the instructions for that unit. Simply placing the downloaded archive on the USB drive is insufficient. Installation behaviour, interruption and resumption depend on the system.

Where updating occurs gradually, leave the medium connected for the period specified by the manufacturer. Licence requirements still apply to USB updates.

Online updates without a physical medium

Supported navigation can update maps over an internet connection. Availability depends on the system, region and relevant services or entitlements.

Online updating may cover selected areas while a complete map package is installed another way. Coverage and data scope are not automatically identical.

Account sign-in or working in-car internet alone does not establish that navigation is active or that all map updates are authorised.

Why one file size and card capacity cannot apply to all systems

Map packages can contain tens of gigabytes. Around 30 GB is a possible example, not a universal update size. Downloaded archives and extracted data also differ in size, and the computer needs space for both.

Some packages no longer fit a 32 GB medium. SD-card or USB-drive type, capacity, file system and folder placement must follow the relevant instructions. A single formatting procedure for every navigation system would be incorrect.

I want to activate navigation

The update method depends on the navigation unit actually installed, its software, region and licence entitlement. The same system name or similar controls do not establish the same procedure. The role of the storage medium also matters: it may supply maps during everyday use or serve only for installation.

Navigation for the selected brand

Availability depends on the market, equipment and infotainment actually installed. The following describes verification routes, rather than guaranteeing availability for every vehicle of a brand.

Volkswagen

Supported Ready 2 Discover systems can gain built-in navigation as an Upgrade through Volkswagen Connect Shop or In-Car Shop. The applicable offer permanently enables offline navigation; online functions depend on the VW Connect Plus licence. Other generations require their own activation check.

Map packages and instructions are available through myVolkswagen after assigning the vehicle. Depending on the system, updates use an original map SD card, an installation SD card, USB or an online connection. Free downloads are not guaranteed for every unit.

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles

This brand also offers navigation unlocking for supported Ready 2 Discover configurations through Volkswagen Connect Webshop or In-Car Shop. The offer must be available for the particular vehicle; it does not apply automatically to every commercial-vehicle infotainment system.

For maps, identify the exact system and prescribed medium. Traffic information and other connected features depend separately on available VW Connect / VW Connect Plus services and their validity.

Audi

Applicable navigation preparations may require an original activation document and the manufacturer's ODIS Service procedure. Other supported systems offer MMI Navigation plus through Functions on Demand in myAudi, for the period specified in the offer. These activation routes are not interchangeable.

myAudi also provides a route to check map availability and entitlement. Some new releases require a separate licence, so the data medium alone may not suffice. Audi connect Navigation & Infotainment features depend on the system and contract.

Škoda

Built-in navigation may already be included in the equipment. Selected infotainment systems allow it to be added through Functions on Demand. Equipment and the current vehicle-specific offer determine which option applies.

The Škoda Update Portal finds maps using the VIN. Instructions distinguish an original operational map SD card from an installation SD card or USB; supported systems also offer online updates. Škoda Connect traffic data and any Traffication app are checked separately from the navigation function itself.

SEAT

First check that the unit has built-in navigation and the appropriate entitlement. Registering or renewing SEAT CONNECT alone does not establish that navigation has been enabled in a unit which previously lacked it.

The manufacturer specifies MapCare or SEAT CONNECT conditions for applicable map updates. Do not overwrite the original map SD card without checking entitlement. Online traffic information and route calculation may be included in the applicable CONNECT Plus package; check its included term and renewal in the account.

CUPRA

The specific navigation system and its licence are decisive. CUPRA CONNECT offers distinguish equipment with navigation from equipment without it; registration alone does not confirm that navigation can be added to every unit by software.

The official CUPRA update portal offers map data and instructions. SD, USB or online transfer depends on the system. Navigation services and traffic information in the applicable CUPRA CONNECT Plus offer are assessed separately, including validity and regional availability.

Navigation included in the equipment or purchased online

Some equipment configurations already include built-in navigation, so the owner does not purchase a separate activation. Navigation included in the equipment is more accurate than promising all navigation services free forever.

Supported infotainment systems allow navigation to be purchased and activated through the manufacturer's online shop or the in-car shop. After sign-in and vehicle assignment, the current offer shows available functions, price and validity. Actual equipment and supported software are decisive; a shop icon alone does not confirm that navigation can be added.

Buying online does not necessarily mean navigation needs a permanent internet connection. For the applicable Ready 2 Discover Upgrade, Volkswagen specifies permanent access to built-in navigation with offline maps. Online traffic information, online route calculation and online map updates have separate VW Connect Plus conditions.

Other offers may have a limited licence or subscription term. Before ordering, identify whether the purchase covers the navigation function, a new map release or connected services.

Navigation activation and map updates are separate

Activating the navigation function enables built-in navigation where the unit has the appropriate preparation and the manufacturer offers an official activation route. Depending on the system, this may require an original activation document and the prescribed ODIS Service procedure, or purchasing the function in the manufacturer's shop.

Entitlement to a new map release may require a separate licence. Navigation can work with its current database yet need a purchased activation document for newer maps. Downloading the files does not replace that licence.

Updating map data changes the database of roads, addresses and other navigation information. It does not automatically update the infotainment software, which has its own manufacturer procedure.

Subscriptions for online traffic information or other Connect services also have their own conditions. They do not automatically activate navigation or authorise every map release.

Traffic information and Car2X

Online traffic information and hazard warnings

Connected navigation services can supplement stored maps with current information about queues, accidents or closures. Supported navigation uses it for route calculation and arrival estimates. Depending on equipment, online destination search and filling or charging station information may also be available. Functions depend on the service, connection and region.

Navigation included in the equipment and an introductory free period of online services are different things. Connected services may require paid renewal when the included period ends. Check scope and expiry in the manufacturer's account or shop for the actual vehicle.

Separate features or apps can also deliver shared hazard warnings. Škoda Traffication, for example, warns of selected traffic risks in supported infotainment systems and can operate without active destination guidance. App availability, required services and any subscription need their own checks.

Car2X means communication between the vehicle and its surroundings. Volkswagen’s direct Car2X system exchanges warnings between compatible vehicles and road infrastructure using WLANp / ITS-G5, without needing a mobile network. It requires the appropriate equipment; purchasing navigation alone does not establish that Car2X is fitted.

The applicable cloud-based Traffication warnings use an online connection. App availability alone does not establish that direct Car2X communication through WLANp / ITS-G5 is fitted.

  • Photos of system information, map version and any error message.
  • Details of your current CD, DVD or SD card, if fitted.
