DSG or S tronic fault warning, or the gearbox will not select a gear

  • DSG or S tronic fault warning, or the gearbox will not select a gear

Back to driver advice ←

What to do now

Stop instruction or unreliable drive
Stop safely, do not continue driving and call roadside assistance. Follow the full message and your vehicle manual.
Reverse is unavailable or gear changes are restricted
The exact message and your vehicle manual determine what to do next. If it is unclear, stop safely first and ask roadside assistance for guidance.
The vehicle does not engage drive in D or R, or drive cuts out
Do not try to force engagement by pressing the accelerator or repeatedly moving the selector.

Once safely stopped, record the full message, the selected position and the circumstances in which the gearbox stopped shifting.

How the workshop checks the cause

Depending on the version, the workshop compares the selector request with the position and engaged gear reported by the control unit. If it registers the request but does not engage the gear or transmit drive, further checks can use gearbox speed and clutch operating data. If it does not register the position, the selector signal and how it reaches the control unit are checked. A fault code alone does not identify a part that must be replaced.
