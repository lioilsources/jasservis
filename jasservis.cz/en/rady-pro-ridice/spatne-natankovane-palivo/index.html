Wrong fuel in the tank: what should you do now?

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Wrong fuel in the tank: what should you do now?

Back to driver advice ←

What to do now

You are already driving
Stop safely as soon as possible, switch off the engine and do not restart it. Call roadside assistance.
You are stopped and the ignition has already been on or the engine has run
Switch off the engine and the ignition. If you are still at the pump, stop refuelling and tell the station staff. Do not start the vehicle again. Tell roadside assistance what happened after refuelling.
You are at the pump and have not switched on the vehicle
Stop refuelling. Do not switch on the ignition or start the engine. In a hybrid, do not activate drive readiness. Inform the staff.

In a hybrid, the combustion engine can start automatically when the car is ready to drive. Arrange any move with the staff and roadside assistance. Do not move the vehicle under its own power.

Have the receipt or a photograph of the fuel label ready and call for professional assistance. Do not try to correct the mix by adding more fuel or an additive without specific instructions for your vehicle.

Breakdown on the road

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

Which vehicles this advice covers

Volkswagen Group vehicles with a combustion engine, including hybrids. The specific fuel system and the manufacturer's procedure for that vehicle determine the scope of the subsequent inspection and repair.

Before starting and after setting off are different situations

The workshop needs to know whether the ignition was switched on, whether starting was attempted and whether the engine actually ran. Simply saying ‘I did not start it’ is therefore not enough. On some models, the fuel pump is activated as soon as a door is opened; also explain what happened with the vehicle after refuelling.

An engine that runs or sounds normal does not confirm that the system has escaped damage. Depending on the vehicle, removing the wrong fuel may be sufficient, or further inspection and repair may be needed. The scope cannot be determined remotely from the number of litres added alone.

Petrol in a diesel vehicle, diesel in a petrol vehicle

Using the wrong fuel can damage the fuel system and the engine. For example, the diesel section of the CUPRA Formentor 11/2022 manual explicitly prohibits starting after refuelling with the wrong fuel and calls for professional assistance. The SEAT Mii manual gives a similar warning about diesel in a petrol vehicle, even in small amounts.

Differences between permitted petrol grades or their octane ratings are a separate issue from mixing up petrol and diesel. Check the vehicle's fuel label and manual. If in doubt, first establish exactly which fuel was added; do not rely on the pump nozzle colour or the commercial name of a premium fuel.

What to tell roadside assistance and the workshop

  • The VIN, model, engine variant and required fuel.
  • Which fuel was added and how much, the approximate fuel level beforehand and the receipt.
  • Whether the ignition was switched on, whether starting was attempted, how long the engine ran and how far the vehicle travelled.
  • The exact message on the display and any changes in engine operation.
  • The vehicle's location and any restrictions on moving or loading it.
