The infotainment is not working correctly

How the cause of individual function failures is investigated

Which vehicles this advice applies to

Radio, multimedia and related functions in Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT and CUPRA vehicles. Design, connections and available remedies vary by model, system generation and equipment.

What to do now

Assess important functions separately

If the camera or parking information fails, check the surroundings directly and do not rely on those aids being available. Address other warnings or functions that cannot be controlled according to the exact message and the vehicle's handbook. Do not allow infotainment operation to distract you while driving.

Restarting and a factory reset have different purposes

Restart the system according to its handbook. Restored operation does not confirm that the cause has been removed. A factory reset may erase the configuration and unlink the user from the vehicle.

During update installation, follow the manufacturer's instructions and do not interfere by trying restarts or disconnecting the power supply. Availability and installation methods vary: some updates are remote, others are workshop updates.

What the symptom may mean

Infotainment freezing or restarting, a black or flickering display, loss of sound and phone connection problems can occur on various Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT and CUPRA models. Depending on the version, navigation, the rear camera image and online services may also be affected.

The cause is determined from the particular system and symptom. It may involve software, a connected phone, the power supply or a system component. We first distinguish a failure of the entire infotainment from a single function, then select the repair according to the results.

Infotainment consists of several cooperating parts

Depending on the version, the system consists of a main unit, display and other components. A fault record in the main unit does not in itself prove that the unit is damaged. The cause may also lie in the display, its installation or another related part of the system.

A separate display can also have its own software. The actual system design is therefore decisive, rather than just which screen shows the problem.

When an update may help

Updates intended for a particular system and its software may address issues such as restarts, loss of picture or sound, or phone connection stability. The improvements vary between updates.

An update will not fix every fault; it must match the vehicle and the findings. Even a software-related cause does not automatically mean that a separate repair by updating is available for that system.

Why we also look for other causes

An incorrect navigation position may relate to an aerial connection, and loss of sound may involve a separate amplifier. This is a reason to check the relevant parts and their connections, rather than automatically replacing the main unit.

The power supply is checked as well. On some vehicles, low charge in the 12 V battery can cause a controlled infotainment shutdown while the engine is off. This is different from repeated restarts while driving.

Bluetooth and app integration are different functions

A phone may handle calls over Bluetooth while CarPlay or Android Auto does not work. Depending on the version, app integration uses a data-capable USB connection or a wireless connection that also uses Wi-Fi. It depends on the functions supported by the phone and system and the necessary permissions.

Camera image and parking information

When parking, distinguish a missing rear camera image, frozen display controls and a message from the parking system itself. A display failure does not yet prove a faulty camera or failure of all parking functions.

On some versions, the infotainment software can also affect availability of the camera image. In other cases, the cause is sought in the relevant part of the parking system. The conclusion is based on the particular symptom and inspection of the vehicle.

Online service failures

An online service may be unavailable even though the radio and phone calls work. It is necessary to determine whether the internet connection, a particular service, the user account or the entire infotainment is failing. Checks cover the link between the vehicle and account, any required user verification, privacy settings and service availability for that equipment.

Depending on the version, a separate communication unit is also involved in online connectivity. Updating it is a different operation from updating the infotainment. Navigation maps are also separate from system software; each update may have its own conditions and method of release.

What to tell the workshop

For a quicker discussion, have the VIN, current mileage and a brief description of the requested work or fault ready. We confirm the appointment and scope of work by agreement.

What to record before visiting the workshop

Report when the problem occurs, what still works and how often it repeats. A connection with a phone, a particular function, an update or other work is important. Note the exact message only after stopping safely.

Also report whether the problem occurs without a connected phone or other external equipment. An accurate description of repeatable behaviour is more useful for diagnosis than a part declared faulty in advance.

Distinguish an unsuccessful connection, call or sound dropouts and a restart of the whole system after connecting a phone. Tell the workshop the phone model, its operating system version, the connection method and whether the problem also occurs without the phone.

How the cause is checked

What diagnosis should establish

The starting point is to identify the exact system, including its hardware and software, and capture its behaviour during the failure. Depending on the problem found, checks cover diagnostic records, the power supply, communication and the affected parts. The workshop also checks whether the manufacturer offers an appropriate solution for that vehicle. An empty fault memory does not in itself rule out a problem.

Depending on the identified cause, the software may need changing, a connection repairing or the relevant component replacing. The repair is not selected simply because the display freezes or shows stripes.
