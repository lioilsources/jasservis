Recalls: how to check whether your car is affected

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Recalls: how to check whether your car is affected

Back to driver advice ←

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

The same model and year of manufacture do not necessarily mean the same recall. Check the specific VIN and the scope of the published check; a result for one campaign does not rule out other outstanding campaigns.

Which vehicles this advice covers

Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT, CUPRA and Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles. The links below lead to official options for customers in the Czech Republic; for an imported vehicle, also state its original market.

What to do now

Have the VIN from the vehicle or registration certificate ready. On the official page, first check whether it covers all available campaigns or only a particular issue, such as Takata airbags.

If your vehicle is affected, contact the brand partner specified. Confirm the next steps and any restrictions on using the vehicle. Do not postpone a stop-driving instruction until your next routine service appointment.

Official checking options for the Czech Republic

The public pages of the individual brands differ in scope. If you cannot find the relevant check or the result is unclear, ask a brand partner or the brand's Czech representative to check the specific VIN.

  • Volkswagen — An overview of selected campaigns and related checks. It is not described as a complete list of all campaigns.
  • Audi — A Czech VIN check for Takata airbags. Ask a brand partner to check other campaigns.
  • Škoda — Separate checks for specific recalls, Takata airbags and EA 189 software. Always read the scope of the check you select.
  • SEAT — A Czech page linking to a Takata campaign check. The result applies to that campaign.
  • CUPRA — The official partner map for a VIN-based enquiry. This is a way to contact a partner, not a public VIN checker.
  • Volkswagen Užitkové vozy — An overview of selected campaigns for commercial models; do not confuse it with the overview for Volkswagen passenger cars.

A negative result for one check does not confirm the status of all campaigns

A check limited to Takata airbags says nothing about other issues. Manufacturers also point out that they update the lists of affected vehicles. Save both the result and the date of the check. For a used or imported vehicle, ask for confirmation of currently outstanding campaigns; do not infer completion solely from a general sales advert or an old service document.

A recall and a routine update provide different information

Do not confuse a safety recall, another service campaign and a routine software update offer. The specific campaign designation and the manufacturer's instructions for the VIN are what matter; an update being available in the infotainment system does not tell you whether the vehicle has an outstanding recall.

What to confirm before your visit

For example, the Czech information on replacing Takata airbags states that replacement is free of charge. This does not automatically apply to unrelated repairs or another service offer.

  • The VIN, exact campaign designation and whether it is still outstanding for this vehicle.
  • What the owner should do until the repair and whether the manufacturer restricts vehicle use.
  • Repair availability, the appointment and the work covered by the campaign; agree any unrelated work separately.
  • After completion, a document identifying the campaign and confirmation that it has been closed for the VIN.
