Service and maintenance intervals

Service and maintenance intervals

The service interval depends on the exact vehicle, equipment, operating conditions and current service configuration. Treat the oil service and inspection dates separately.

J&S SERVIS recommends replacing the engine oil and filter no later than after 1 year or 15,000 km, whichever comes first. If the manufacturer specifies a shorter interval, the shorter interval applies.
Fixed interval and LongLife
RegimeHow the date is determinedImportant
Fixed intervalA predetermined time and mileage; whichever limit is reached first.Uses a fixed service display and the corresponding oil regime.
LongLife / flexibleThe vehicle calculates the oil-service date from operating data, subject to the manufacturer’s maximum limit.LongLife oil alone neither activates the regime nor automatically extends the interval.
What QI and QG codes mean

QI codes are PR codes for the service display. These are code meanings, not J&S SERVIS recommendations:

QI

CodeService display
QI0Without a service display.
QI1Fixed: 5,000 km or 1 year.
QI2Fixed: 7,500 km or 1 year.
QI3Fixed: 10,000 km or 1 year.
QI4Fixed: 15,000 km or 1 year.
QI6Flexible service; the vehicle calculates the date, no later than the manufacturer limit for the exact version.

QG codes mainly occur in older documentation. Their use changed by production period, so they cannot establish a current service plan without context:

QG

CodeMeaning in older documentation
QG0Without extended service intervals.
QG1With extended service intervals (LongLife).
QG2In older service schedules, fixed time- or distance-based servicing. Any change of regime must be checked for the particular vehicle.

Newer vehicles distinguish the service regime with QI codes; QG may describe other equipment such as an oil sensor. Use the data sticker, VIN, documentation and actual vehicle configuration.

Where to find my service interval
Vehicle data sticker
PR codes may appear on the vehicle data sticker or in service documentation. Form and location are not identical on every vehicle.
VIN and manufacturer catalogue
The complete VIN can identify the production version and matching service plan.
Current vehicle setting
The instrument panel or infotainment shows the currently configured due date. It does not prove that a previous recoding was correct.
Options for changing the interval setting
  • First verify that the manufacturer permits both fixed and flexible regimes for the exact vehicle.
  • Use oil approved for the selected regime and engine.
  • Recode the service display diagnostically under the manufacturer procedure; merely resetting it need not change the regime.
  • Record the change and new due date in the service history.
  • When changing the service regime, do not exceed any binding time or mileage limit specified by the manufacturer.
Servicing electric and hybrid vehicles
Model groupEngine oilService and exceptions
MEB battery-electric vehicles: Volkswagen ID., Škoda Enyaq/Elroq, CUPRA Born/Tavascan, Audi Q4 e-tronNo combustion engine and therefore no engine-oil change.For example, Škoda Enyaq / Enyaq Coupé and the corresponding Volkswagen ID. models require a regular service inspection no later than every 2 years, without a mileage limit. The exact vehicle service schedule governs; individual operations can have their own interval.
Other Audi battery-electric vehicles: e-tron / Q8 e-tron and e-tron GTNo combustion-engine oil service.Design differs from MEB; use the exact model plan. Gear fluids and cooling circuits are not engine oil.
Plug-in hybrids: GTE, eHybrid, TFSI e, iV and e-HYBRIDHave a combustion engine; its oil and filter follow the prescribed regime.High-voltage tasks are separate. Frequent cold combustion-engine use can justify the shorter recommended interval.
Mild hybrids and other hybrids with a combustion engineEngine-oil changes remain necessary.Electrification does not cancel oil service. The exact engine, VIN and service plan govern.

A model name or electric drive does not define a universal interval. Verify exceptions for production period, market, powertrain, equipment and service actions by VIN.

Oil change versus service inspection
RegimeImportant
Oil serviceEngine-oil and oil-filter replacement, with its own date based on regime, engine and operation.
Service inspectionVehicle checks and other prescribed work. Its date can differ from the oil service.
Separate operationsBrake fluid, filters, spark plugs, transmissions, all-wheel drive and other items can have their own time or mileage conditions.

Completing one operation does not automatically complete the others. Each applicable date must be recorded and configured correctly after service.
