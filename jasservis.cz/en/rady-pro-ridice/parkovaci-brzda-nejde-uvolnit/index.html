Electronic parking brake will not release

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Electronic parking brake will not release

Back to driver advice ←

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

If the parking brake will not release, the brake itself is not necessarily faulty. It is important to distinguish its operating conditions, the vehicle power supply and the exact instrument panel message.

Which vehicles this advice covers

Vehicles equipped with an electronic parking brake. Operation, automatic functions and fault handling depend on the specific version.

What to do now

Do not try to drive off against the applied brake using drive power or forcefully tow the vehicle. Before attempting release, secure it against unintended movement as specified in the handbook. If it cannot be secured safely or released normally, arrange professional assistance.

Do not continue driving if a STOP instruction is displayed or braking effectiveness has deteriorated. Photograph the exact message.

Parking brake, Auto Hold and the selector

For the workshop, distinguish whether the parking brake symbol is illuminated, HOLD is active or the selector position cannot be changed. Auto Hold and the parking brake have different functions; their operation and automatic interactions vary by vehicle. A P marking alone does not identify the cause of the problem.

An operating condition or a fault?

Check only the normal operating conditions described in your vehicle handbook. For example, it may require the brake pedal to be pressed. Do not confuse normal operation with an emergency release or service mode.

CUPRA Born 07/2025: failure to release may be associated with a connected charging plug or a discharged 12V battery. This example applies to that edition.

What to prepare when arranging assistance

  • VIN, model and current mileage.
  • A photograph of the complete message and warning lights.
  • Whether the problem appeared after parking, charging or a repair.
  • Whether the brake control responds and the selector position can be changed.
  • Whether the vehicle is on a slope and any wheel is locked.

When transport is needed

Tell the recovery service in advance that the parking brake will not release. The handling procedure and any emergency release must match the vehicle and fault. Disconnecting the battery, removing brake actuators or interfering with wiring cannot be recommended as a universal procedure.
