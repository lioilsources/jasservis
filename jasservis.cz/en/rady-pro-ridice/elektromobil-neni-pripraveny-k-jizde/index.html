Electric vehicle or plug-in hybrid will not become ready to drive

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Electric vehicle or plug-in hybrid will not become ready to drive

Back to driver advice ←

What to check while stationary

Check the usual conditions for activating the drive system in your owner's manual. Depending on the car, this may require a recognised key, pressing the brake pedal or setting the selector to the specified position. If a charging cable is connected, follow the manual when ending the charge and unplugging it. Do not force the connector out.

If the display tells you to stop driving or a fault persists, follow the displayed instructions and call roadside assistance. Avoid trying to clear the warning by repeatedly switching the car off and on. Leave the high-voltage system alone.

If the car does not become ready to drive, secure it against unintended movement and make a note of the message on the display. Some models show READY or another indicator. The drive system may be active while the combustion engine is off, so use the indicator specified for your vehicle.

How a workshop checks the cause

The workshop compares the exact message and when it appeared with stored faults and the conditions the car checks before enabling the drive system. Depending on the model, that may include the brake pedal signal, selector position, key recognition or charging cable status. Those results guide checks of the 12 V supply and any faults in the drive system. A lit display alone cannot confirm that the 12 V supply is working properly.
