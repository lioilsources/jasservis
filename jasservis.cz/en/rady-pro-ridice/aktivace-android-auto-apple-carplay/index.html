Android Auto and Apple CarPlay

Want to use phone apps on your vehicle’s screen? Android Auto and CarPlay can be activated with an original licence where the equipment supports it.

What to send us

  • The vehicle VIN.
  • Photos of the main menu and infotainment system information.
  • Your phone model and whether you want a wired or wireless connection.

Arrange a vehicle check

We confirm activation availability and price after checking. Both the phone and infotainment must support a wireless connection.

Which infotainment systems can be activated?

Infotainment and conditions for the selected brand

Volkswagen

For MIB2, the main candidates are supported versions of Composition Media and Discover Media, as well as selected MIB2 Entry units. The manufacturer distinguishes between activation documents for different unit variants.

MIB3 systems include names such as Composition, Ready 2 Discover, Discover Media and Discover Pro. Workshop activation documents exist for selected units; some vehicles also allow App-Connect to be purchased through the In-Car Shop. The actual offer depends on the vehicle and market.

The Discover Pro name alone does not confirm that a particular activation document can be used. First, the existing equipment and the manufacturer's licensing route for that unit must be checked.

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles

The function is also called App-Connect. Commercial vehicles use different radios and navigation systems depending on generation and equipment, such as Composition Media, Composition Colour or Discover Media.

Passenger-car Volkswagen conditions cannot automatically be applied to Caddy, Transporter, Multivan, California, Crafter or Amarok. The platform and installed system must be checked. For example, wireless CarPlay support on a particular version does not establish the same support for Android Auto.

Škoda

On older supported systems, SmartLink/SmartLink+ licence activation applies to selected versions of Bolero, Amundsen and Swing+. The activation document's application list includes suitable Octavia III, Rapid and Yeti versions; production restrictions apply to some other models.

For the relevant Bolero and Amundsen versions, the manufacturer specifies Fabia III production from calendar week 22/2016 and Superb III production up to calendar week 30/2020. The particular infotainment's other conditions must also be met. Swing and Swing+ must not automatically be treated as the same radio when ordering a licence.

For selected MIB3 units in Fabia IV, Scala, Kamiq and Octavia IV, the manufacturer distinguishes:

  • activating wired SmartLink where the appropriate preparation is present;
  • activating wireless SmartLink on a supported unit;
  • upgrading an already active wired SmartLink function to wireless.

For the relevant initial MIB3 activations, Škoda specifies preparation code 9WC; for upgrading an already active wired SmartLink function to wireless, it specifies code 9WT. The exact infotainment version and all other document conditions must also match. These PR codes are not a universal guarantee that activation is possible.

These options have separate licensing conditions. The relevant MIB3 documents are not intended for Amundsen and Columbus with equipment code 8AR, and further restrictions apply to unit versions and production dates. Their use is explicitly excluded for selected newer units from 2024. Neither the vehicle model nor the MIB3 designation alone is sufficient to select a document.

Excluding a particular document does not mean that the vehicle cannot use Android Auto or CarPlay. The function may already be included, or different conditions may apply.

SEAT

Full Link is found on selected Media System Plus, Navi System and Navi System Plus systems, and on supported Media System Color / Entry Plus versions in some vehicles.

The manufacturer distinguishes activation for the older MIB2 generation from MIB3. An MIB2 document is not a universal MIB3 activation. Some older vehicles may also require a suitable USB socket in addition to the licence. Wired and wireless connectivity must also be distinguished.

CUPRA

CUPRA also uses Full Link, or Wireless Full Link for supported wireless connections. Subsequent Full Link activation is documented for older Ateca versions, for example with Navi System Plus; newer systems have different connection options according to equipment.

Genuine Full Link activations are also offered for selected MIB3 units, for example in the relevant Ateca and Leon versions. The unit and PR equipment determine which document applies. The catalogue specifies a USB connection for these activations; an activation offer alone therefore does not confirm wireless operation.

Conditions must be checked for the particular infotainment and vehicle. Sharing the Full Link name does not mean that any activation document can be used for CUPRA or that all models have the same device generation.

Audi

Audi smartphone interface is assessed on supported MIB2-generation and newer MMI versions, including some MMI radio plus and MMI navigation plus systems. The MMI name alone does not establish compatibility.

On some older vehicles, the genuine retrofit includes a suitable USB interface and wiring, followed by activation according to the manufacturer's procedure. Purchasing a licence is therefore not always the only requirement.

On selected newer Audis, the interface can be purchased through Functions on Demand in myAudi. Availability is tied to the vehicle, equipment and market; the purchased function's duration may also vary. The paper activation document and workshop activation route cannot automatically be assumed in this case.

What does the function do and what is it called?

Would you like to use navigation, music, calls or messages from your phone on your car's display? Android Auto and Apple CarPlay connect a compatible phone to the infotainment system and make selected apps available through the vehicle's controls.

Some vehicles already have this equipment activated; others allow it to be enabled under licence. Where the manufacturer offers activation by an activation document for the particular configuration, the correct genuine licence must be purchased and the prescribed procedure carried out using ODIS Service. This does not automatically apply to every radio or infotainment generation.

We first check the VIN, the infotainment actually installed and its equipment. Only then can the appropriate procedure, required parts and price be confirmed.

Activation depends on the VIN, equipment and the actual infotainment hardware and software. The brand or radio name alone does not confirm compatibility.

Android Auto is intended for compatible Android phones; Apple CarPlay is for compatible iPhones. Available apps and functions also depend on the phone, its operating system and market support. Not every app on your phone will appear on the vehicle display.

Phones, cables and wireless connections

Wired and wireless connections are different equipment

For a wired connection, the infotainment, phone, USB port and cable must all support the required data transfer. A socket that charges a phone is not necessarily suitable for Android Auto or CarPlay.

Wireless connectivity requires support from both the vehicle and phone and also uses Wi-Fi. A working Bluetooth hands-free connection does not establish this support. Some versions support wireless CarPlay and wireless Android Auto differently.

Activating the wired function does not automatically provide the wireless version. A standard USB adapter that converts working wired Android Auto or CarPlay into a wireless connection requires a supported, active wired function in the vehicle. Installing a separate interface is a different type of retrofit.

Phone requirements and checking wireless compatibility

Both the phone and the actual infotainment must support wireless projection. Bluetooth hands-free pairing or the presence of Wi-Fi alone does not confirm this capability.

Android Auto

Google’s current help page specifies Android 11 or later, 5 GHz Wi-Fi support and an active mobile data plan. Android Auto is unavailable on Android Go. Additional requirements apply to in-car 5 GHz Wi-Fi use in the EU; check support for your exact model with the phone manufacturer.

Find the system version in Settings → About phone, sometimes under Software information. You can search Settings for Android Auto. Menu names vary by manufacturer. Google provides technical requirements here, rather than a complete list of phone model names.

Android Auto – check current phone requirements

Apple CarPlay

The Apple CarPlay page lists compatible iPhones near the bottom. This confirms CarPlay support; the actual vehicle system must also support wireless operation. Keep iOS updated within your phone’s supported software versions.

Enable Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Siri. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay and select your vehicle. When setting up wireless CarPlay, also check that Auto-Join is enabled for the CarPlay network in Wi-Fi settings.

CarPlay – view the list of compatible iPhones

The first wireless connection

Set up pairing while parked, following the infotainment and phone prompts. The initial wireless Android Auto pairing uses Bluetooth; keep Wi-Fi and Location Services enabled during setup too. Some vehicles use a USB cable for the initial CarPlay setup and then offer a wireless connection.

When the phone needs a cable

If either the phone or infotainment does not support wireless projection, but both support wired Android Auto or CarPlay, use a suitable data USB cable and the appropriate USB port in the vehicle.

A cable alone cannot add support to a phone or infotainment that does not support Android Auto or CarPlay at all. Phone requirements change; check the current requirements before ordering activation.

How does original activation work?

How activation through ODIS Service works

The vehicle and infotainment are identified first. Hardware, software, available functions and relevant faults are checked. The assessment also covers the required USB interface and whether the infotainment or its accessories have previously been replaced.

Where the appropriate genuine licence is available, the correct activation document is selected. It contains the information required to enable the licensed function; it is not merely a printed phone-pairing guide.

The prescribed procedure is then carried out in ODIS Service with the required manufacturer online access. Depending on the system, this may include software adaptation or installing the necessary hardware first. Vehicle power is maintained as specified in the relevant procedure.

Finally, the activation result and connection to a compatible phone are checked. Keep the activation document with the vehicle records; a licence tied to one vehicle is not a transferable key for another car.

Details of the information needed before booking

What to provide for checking your vehicle

Please send us:

  • the vehicle VIN;
  • a photo of the infotainment's main menu;
  • a photo of the system information and software version screen;
  • any message displayed when opening the phone connection function;
  • your phone model and whether you require a wired or wireless connection;
  • information about previous infotainment replacement or modification.

System information is usually found in the infotainment settings; the exact location varies by device. Do not enter service or hidden menus for this purpose. If you cannot find the information, we can identify the system during diagnostics.

The function used to work but has stopped

Did the function previously work? Check the fault first

If Android Auto or CarPlay previously worked in this vehicle, buying a new licence may not be the solution. The cause may be the phone, cable, settings, software or activation status after service work.

Signing in to the brand's app does not replace missing infotainment equipment either. Android Auto or CarPlay smartphone integration and the vehicle's remote online services are different functions.

At J&S SERVIS, we first check your vehicle's actual configuration and the available official activation route. We confirm the required work and price before ordering the licence.
