Engine warning light on or flashing. What now?

  • Engine warning light on or flashing. What now?

Back to driver advice ←

What to do now

The display says STOP or you cannot control the vehicle safely
Stop safely and do not continue driving.
The warning light is flashing
Stop safely. Once stopped, check the exact message and your vehicle handbook, even if the engine seems to be running normally. If the vehicle jerks or loses power, or if you are unsure what to do next, call roadside assistance.
The warning light stays on and the vehicle behaves normally
Follow your vehicle manual and arrange a diagnostic check.

The light may briefly come on as a normal check when you switch on the ignition. If it stays on after starting or comes on while driving, read the full message as soon as it is safe. The engine warning light and the separate EPC warning can have different meanings.

What to tell the workshop

Note whether the light was steady or flashing, when it appeared and whether the car jerked or lost power. Save the exact message and any fault report. A code alone does not identify a part to replace. Only photograph the message once safely stopped.

How the cause is checked

Fault codes and any available operating data from the moment the fault was logged should be saved during diagnosis. The symptoms then guide the choice of a targeted test or measurement. The results help determine what to repair or replace.
