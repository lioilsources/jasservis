Warning lights and driver messages
Engine, lubrication and cooling

Glow plug lamp – preheating before starting · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Glow plug lamp – preheating before starting

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On a diesel engine it lights during preheating. This normal state before starting is not itself a fault.

Recommended action

Wait for the lamp to go out before starting, as instructed in the vehicle’s manual.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Diesel preheating and management

Yellow / amber · Information / warning depending on state

Meaning

Brief illumination before starting can be normal preheating. Illumination or flashing while running can indicate an engine management fault.

Recommended action

Follow the message and arrange inspection. The symbol does not automatically identify defective glow plugs.

Key indication

Brief illumination before starting; illumination or flashing while running.

Engine oil pressure

Red · Warning

Meaning

The warning indicates insufficient engine oil pressure. A correct oil level alone does not confirm adequate lubrication pressure.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. Do not leave it idling. Further steps, including any restart, must follow the specific vehicle manual; if unsure, obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Do not confuse this with a yellow oil level warning. The source describes a conditional procedure after topping up; this is not general permission to restart.

Combustion engine cooling system

Red · Warning

Meaning

May indicate overheating, an incorrect coolant level or a cooling system fault.

Recommended action

Stop safely, switch off the engine and follow the vehicle message. Check coolant as instructed only after safe cooling. Do not open a hot expansion tank.

Key indication

Visible coolant in the reservoir does not by itself resolve the warning. This entry does not cover electric-drive cooling.

Engine management and emissions – MIL

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

The control system detected an engine management or emissions fault. The lamp alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Distinguish steady illumination from flashing. On relevant versions, flashing can indicate misfiring. If the engine shakes, vibrates or loses power markedly, stop safely and contact professional assistance. Otherwise follow the message and vehicle manual.

Key indication

Clearing a fault code does not remove the cause. This entry does not give universal permission to continue driving.

Diesel particulate filter – DPF

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

On relevant diesel versions it indicates accumulated soot and a need for filter regeneration.

Recommended action

Check regeneration driving conditions in your specific manual. If other warnings appear or engine operation is abnormal, do not follow a universal internet regeneration procedure.

Key indication

No single speed, duration or engine speed applies to all engines. Petrol GPF/OPF requires separate verification.

Brakes and stability systems

ESC / TCS intervention · multiple meanings of the same symbol

ESC / TCS intervention

Yellow / amber · Operating information · Flashing

Meaning

Flashing indicates that stability or traction control is intervening. It does not by itself indicate a fault.

Recommended action

Adapt speed and driving style to available grip and traffic conditions. The system cannot overcome physical limits.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

ESC fault or system-induced deactivation

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

A lamp that stays on after the initial check may indicate a fault or system-induced deactivation. This differs from flashing during intervention.

Recommended action

Read the message and follow the cited manual. If the lamp remains on, arrange professional inspection; do not rely on stability control being available.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Brake system

Red · Warning

Meaning

Depending on the version, the symbol may relate to an applied parking brake, low brake fluid or a brake system fault.

Recommended action

The exact symbol and message are decisive. If the warning remains after releasing the parking brake or the vehicle reports a brake fault, stop safely and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Topping up brake fluid does not repair the cause of its loss. Distinguish a fault warning from the normal parking brake indication.

Brake pad wear

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

Brake wear monitoring indicates that the brake pads require inspection.

Recommended action

Arrange brake inspection. The lamp does not reliably indicate the kilometres remaining before replacement.

Key indication

Do not confuse it with a red brake system fault warning.

ABS – anti-lock braking system

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

A fault has been detected in the anti-lock braking system.

Recommended action

Follow the vehicle message and have the brake system inspected. If a red brake warning also appears or braking behaves abnormally, stop safely and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Braking behaviour can change; a yellow ABS lamp does not universally confirm safe onward driving.

Steering, chassis and tyres

Tyre pressure

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

The symbol warns of incorrect tyre pressure. The symbol alone does not identify a particular leak. Fault-flashing behaviour is not documented here; check the model-specific manual.

Recommended action

Stop safely and check tyres and pressures. Store reference values only after proper inspection and adjustment using the manufacturer’s procedure.

Key indication

Acknowledging or resetting the warning does not repair a puncture or leak. Direct sensors and indirect wheel-speed-based monitoring can require different learning procedures.

Airbags and belt tensioners

Yellow / amber · Warning

Meaning

The restraint system reports a fault. Protection in an accident may be restricted.

Recommended action

Arrange professional inspection promptly. Do not attempt repairs to airbags or belt tensioners yourself.

Key indication

Distinguish it from a separate passenger airbag OFF operating indication.

Seat belts

Red · Warning

Meaning

The lamp reminds an occupant to fasten the seat belt.

Recommended action

Ensure all occupants fasten their seat belts correctly before driving. Follow the vehicle’s seat occupancy information.

Key indication

Indications can differ by seat and equipment; the symbol alone is not a diagnosis of a sensor fault.

Headlight range control

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The amber indicator in the cited general Volkswagen UK source indicates a fault in the headlight range control system.

Recommended action

Contact an authorised repairer.

Key indication

Steady amber illumination; the general page does not state a flashing variant. This is specifically headlight range control, not a generic lighting fault or automatic proof of a failed level sensor. Check your vehicle’s owner’s manual for the meaning applicable to your specific vehicle.

Rear fog light

Yellow / amber · Operating information

Meaning

The rear fog light is switched on.

Recommended action

Use it according to visibility and traffic rules and switch it off when no longer needed.

Key indication

This is an operating indication, not an engine fault.

Main-beam control – enabled, white A (Golf)

White · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

In this Golf edition, white A indicates enabled main-beam control. Actual main-beam illumination is confirmed by the separate blue main-beam indicator.

Recommended action

Monitor actual lighting and switch manually if needed. Poorly lit road users, an obscured camera, weather or road layout can restrict automatic operation.

Key indication

Colour is documented by the original white rendition in this exact edition’s symbol overview. The section also describes three confirmation flashes during sensitivity adjustment in some markets; their colour is not independently assumed and the flashes are not represented as a fault.

Take-over steering request

White · Information / warning depending on state · Steady

Meaning

The vehicle asks the driver to take over steering. This does not confirm that the system can handle the situation independently.

Recommended action

Safely take over steering, monitor traffic and follow the exact vehicle message. Be ready to brake.

Key indication

The indicator illuminates white and an accompanying prompt appears; the reviewed chapters do not state flashing. The red request has higher urgency. Association with Travel Assist, Emergency Assist or Lane Assist depends on equipment and the cited manual.

Take over steering immediately

Red · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The vehicle requests immediate driver steering control.

Recommended action

Safely take over steering immediately, monitor traffic and follow the exact vehicle message. Be ready to brake.

Key indication

The indicator illuminates red with an accompanying message; depending on the situation, a sound or steering-wheel vibration follows. The reviewed chapters do not state flashing. Association with Travel Assist, Emergency Assist or Lane Assist depends on equipment and the cited manual.

Lane Assist – unavailable, symbol with exclamation mark

Yellow / amber · Function restricted · Steady

Meaning

The amber Lane Assist symbol with exclamation mark and message means the assistant is unavailable. Camera-view limitations, damage or faults may be responsible; the symbol alone does not identify a failed sensor.

Recommended action

The driver must steer. Follow the manual to inspect and clean the camera view and remove obstructions or stickers. Seek a qualified workshop if the problem persists; follow vehicle messages.

Key indication

The documented state is the steady amber car-between-lanes variant with an exclamation mark and unavailable message: Golf 5H0 11.2020, ID.4 11.2021, Terramar CS 11.25 and Tarraco DE 06.2023. Amber without an exclamation mark may mean corrective intervention, switched-on inactive operation or undetected markings in another precisely cited state; amber alone does not prove a fault.

Battery and electrical system

Electrical system and charging

Red · Warning

Meaning

On a combustion vehicle the warning can indicate a charging or electrical system fault. It does not automatically mean a defective battery.

Recommended action

Read the accompanying message and follow the manual. With additional warnings or restricted functions, stop safely and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

An electric vehicle’s 12 V system needs a separately verified explanation; it must not be described as a conventional alternator failure.

Yellow / amber · Function restricted · Steady

Meaning

The electronic vehicle sound is not working. Other road users may not hear the approaching vehicle.

Recommended action

Drive with particular care and arrange prompt professional inspection as directed by the cited manual and any further messages.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Electric drive – reduced available power

Yellow / amber · Function restricted · Steady

Meaning

Available power is restricted and may decrease further depending on conditions. Charge level or high-voltage battery temperature may be involved; this is not an automatic diagnosis of battery failure.

Recommended action

Follow the message and power display and adapt driving to reduced acceleration. If charge is low, follow the manual and charge the battery. If safe driving is not possible, stop safely and obtain help.

Key indication

No remaining range is guaranteed. Red turtle indications have separate model-specific explanations; changing the colour does not preserve the procedure.

ID.4 – battery depleted, drive shutdown imminent · multiple meanings of the same symbol

ID.4 – battery depleted, drive shutdown imminent

Red · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

While driving, a red turtle with the corresponding message indicates a very low high-voltage battery charge and imminent vehicle immobilisation. Power is reduced.

Recommended action

Pull over and park safely as traffic permits; use a charging station only if it can be reached safely. Follow the full message and manual. Do not infer remaining range from the symbol.

Key indication

ID.4 11/2021 distinguishes a red turtle while driving with a depleted battery from a red turtle before starting when the battery is too cold. This entry concerns depletion while driving; the exact message is decisive.

ID.4 – battery too cold, driving not possible

Red · Warning · Symbol with message

Meaning

On the cited ID.4, the red symbol with this message indicates very low high-voltage battery temperature and insufficient power for normal driving.

Recommended action

Do not continue normal driving. Secure the vehicle safely and follow the exact message and manual; charge the battery if its state of charge is low. Follow the manual for cold-weather battery preparation.

Key indication

This entry applies to the stated ID.4 edition. It is not electric-drive overheating or a universal meaning of a red turtle on every EV.
