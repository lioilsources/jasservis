Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

Enyaq iV – coolant warning

Red · Warning · Steady / flashing

Meaning

The separate coolant symbol lights up or flashes for excessive temperature or low level. Do not confuse it with the combined electrical-system overheating warning.

Recommended action

Stop safely. Check the level only by the safe procedure in the manual after cooling. If the warning persists with the correct level or safe topping-up is not possible, do not continue; obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

For the combined electrical-system overheating warning on page 201: stop, switch off the ignition, do not add coolant and obtain assistance.

Light Assist – automatically activated main beam, blue A · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Light Assist – automatically activated main beam, blue A

Blue · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

In the cited Enyaq, Born and Terramar editions, the blue A symbol denotes automatically activated main beam according to each edition’s own operating instructions. The neutral assistant-enabled indication is a different state.

Recommended action

Watch oncoming/preceding vehicles and other road users. Switch main beam off manually when necessary; an obscured camera or poorly lit road user may not be recognised correctly.

Key indication

Applies only to the listed model editions and equipment. Do not confuse with the Tarraco 06.2023 blue variant, whose own legend is documented separately. Intervene manually if dazzling is possible.

Dynamic Light Assist – automatic main beam with beam adaptation

Blue · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On the specified Enyaq equipment, blue A indicates automatically activated main beam. Dynamic Light Assist additionally adapts the beam to avoid dazzling oncoming/preceding vehicles; the indicator does not guarantee their recognition.

Recommended action

Intervene manually if there is a risk of dazzling. Account for obscured, dirty or failed lamps on other vehicles and the conditions in the actual manual.

Key indication

Only Dynamic Light Assist equipment, not automatically all Enyaqs. The same A shape on ordinary Light Assist does not establish dynamic beam adaptation.
