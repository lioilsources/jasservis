Before a long drive – what to check

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Before a long drive – what to check

Back to driver advice ←

A few days before leaving, check the tyres, fluids, lights and visibility. If a warning or fault has already appeared, you will have time to get it checked.

Checks you can do before leaving

Check cold tyre pressures against the vehicle label for the planned load. Inspect the tread and sidewalls and check that the spare wheel or repair kit is usable. Check the lights, wipers and washers. If you have a tyre repair kit, also check the sealant expiry date.

Check the oil level as described in the manual. Check the coolant with the engine cold and keep the reservoir closed when hot. Make sure you know what roadside assistance is available to you.

When to arrange a workshop check

Have repeated pressure loss, fluid leaks, new vibrations, changes in braking or warning messages checked before you leave. Note when the issue appeared and photograph the exact message.

If the brake pedal suddenly goes hard or sinks while driving, pull over safely and arrange assistance. Brake pedal has gone hard or is sinking.
