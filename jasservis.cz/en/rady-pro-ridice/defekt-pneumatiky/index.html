Tyre puncture: when a repair kit is not enough

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Tyre puncture: when a repair kit is not enough

Back to driver advice ←

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

Pressure loss alone does not determine whether a tyre can be sealed in an emergency. A repair kit is a temporary solution; further driving depends on the damage, the kit used and the result of the prescribed check.

Which vehicles this advice covers

For vehicles equipped with a tyre repair kit. The exact kit, tyre and handbook for the specific vehicle are decisive. This overview does not replace the instructions for using the kit.

What to do now

Stop safely and assess the location. If you cannot work clear of traffic hazards or do not know the procedure, arrange roadside assistance. Do not continue driving if there is severe vibration, a change in steering behaviour or visible sidewall damage.

What to record when arranging assistance

  • The vehicle location and which wheel is affected.
  • Photographs of the tyre, rim and warning message.
  • Whether the tyre has been driven with low pressure or completely flat.
  • Whether sealant has been used, which product and with what result.

When the kit may be unsuitable

Read its restrictions first. For example, the Continental ContiMobilityKit instructions exclude sidewall damage and tyres damaged by driving with insufficient pressure. Also check the sealant’s usable life and whether the kit is suitable for the tyre. Do not pull out a foreign object if the instructions require it to remain in place.

Why simply reinflating the tyre is not enough

Driving with low pressure can cause internal damage that is not visible from outside. Even a small puncture does not automatically mean a straightforward repair. Professional assessment includes removing the tyre from the rim and inspecting its inside. Neither a photograph nor the fact that the tyre now holds pressure confirms that it can be repaired.

What to check before considering further driving

Follow the prescribed inflation procedure, subsequent check and driving restrictions in the specific instructions. Do not use pressure values, compressor running times or permitted driving distances from another kit.

If the prescribed result cannot be achieved or the pressure fails the subsequent check, do not continue driving and seek professional assistance. Stop safely if new vibration or a change in steering behaviour occurs.

Temporary sealing is not a permanent repair

Have the tyre professionally inspected as soon as possible. Before it is removed, tell the tyre workshop about the sealant used and any previous driving with low pressure. Further action must follow the findings and the requirements of the vehicle and tyre manufacturers; replacement may be necessary.
