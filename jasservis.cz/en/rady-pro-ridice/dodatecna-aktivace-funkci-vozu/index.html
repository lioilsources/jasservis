Some functions can be purchased after vehicle delivery. The equipment and manufacturer’s offer for your particular vehicle determine what is available.

What to send us

  • The vehicle VIN and the function you would like.
  • A photo of the function’s offer or displayed message.
  • System information and details of previous modifications.

Arrange a vehicle check

A licence does not add missing hardware. Check availability, price and validity for your vehicle before purchasing.

What does my brand offer?

Offer for the selected brand

Volkswagen

Depending on supported equipment, Upgrades can include:

  • More ambient lighting colours and separately offered lighting effects.
  • Light Assist automatic high-beam switching.
  • Adaptive Cruise Control ACC and traffic sign recognition.
  • Voice control, App-Connect and built-in navigation.
  • On selected electric configurations, an Air Care Climatronic upgrade with two-zone control and further climate functions.

The primary user can view vehicle-specific offers in In-Car Shop or Volkswagen Connect Shop. Climate upgrades and other items are not available on every generation. VW Connect Plus online services are assessed separately.

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles

The manufacturer lists these supported Upgrades:

  • Multicolour ambient lighting and applicable visual effects with the appropriate preparation.
  • Light Assist high-beam assistance.
  • Voice control and navigation unlocking.
  • App-Connect for applicable infotainment systems.

The actual offer in In-Car Shop or Volkswagen Connect Webshop is decisive. Lighting upgrades also need the correct installed lighting equipment. VW Connect Plus is a separate subscription; this offer does not apply automatically to every commercial vehicle.

Audi

Depending on supported equipment, Functions on Demand can include:

  • Matrix LED, the extended lighting package and high-beam assistance.
  • Adaptive cruise control and traffic sign recognition.
  • Parking assistance within the scope of the applicable version.
  • DAB digital radio, Audi virtual cockpit plus and two-zone comfort climate control on applicable configurations.
  • Audi smartphone interface and MMI Navigation plus with the relevant connect package.

myAudi shows availability and usage periods after assigning the actual vehicle. This is not the installation of new headlights, sensors or climate hardware: the appropriate technical preparation must already be present for the purchase.

Škoda

Supported Functions on Demand can include:

  • Additional ambient lighting colours.
  • Extended ambient effects: applicable offers include acceleration-responsive effects in Sport mode, hazard-light synchronisation or open-door signalling.
  • Automatic high-beam control.
  • Traffic sign recognition and adaptive cruise control.
  • Laura voice assistance and navigation.

Check offers in Škoda Connect Shop or the integrated infotainment shop. Basic ambient lighting alone does not confirm upgrade eligibility. Škoda Connect services and their validity are checked separately.

SEAT

The published offer mainly distinguishes CONNECT services and shop content:

  • Online traffic information, route calculation and other functions in the applicable CONNECT Plus package.
  • Remote functions such as status checks or locking, where supported by the vehicle and chosen package.
  • Data packages and apps available in the shop for the actual infotainment.

In My SEAT or the in-car shop, check which services are included and which can be renewed or purchased for a fee. Not every app is paid. A CONNECT subscription does not confirm that ambient lighting or driving assistants can be unlocked; such equipment must be explicitly offered for the vehicle.

CUPRA

Verified purchase options mainly concern services and digital content:

  • The applicable CUPRA CONNECT Plus offer with online traffic and navigation services.
  • Supported remote functions in My CUPRA, including charging and climate control on electrified configurations.
  • Apps and digital items actually offered for the specific infotainment.

Price and included periods depend on the service and vehicle; some items may already be included. A CONNECT subscription or software update alone does not grant new ambient colours, ACC or other equipment activation. Such activation must be verified as a specific product for your vehicle.

Activation requirements and what you are buying

Additional interior lighting colours, lighting assistants and comfort functions can sometimes be purchased after delivery. The manufacturer’s offer for the actual vehicle is decisive.

Availability depends on the VIN, actual equipment, hardware, software and market. A brand overview is not a list of features available in every vehicle.

When a function can be activated

The vehicle needs the appropriate technical preparation and supported software. A sensor, camera, radar or menu entry alone does not confirm activation eligibility. Production date and destination market can also affect the conditions.

What you are buying

  • Vehicle-function licence – enables supported equipment for the period stated in the offer.
  • Online-service subscription – provides traffic data or remote access, for example.
  • Apps and data packages – have their own conditions and possible separate charges.
Ambient lighting upgrades

What an ambient lighting upgrade means

Where the manufacturer offers more colours or lighting effects, this extends the functions of the appropriate installed lighting. A licence does not physically add missing light strips, LED modules or wiring. Additional colours, dynamic effects and fitting more lighting are different options.

Where to check availability and price

Checking availability and price

  1. Sign in to the manufacturer’s account, assign the vehicle by VIN and complete the required user verification.
  2. Open the function or service offer in the app or infotainment. Check the exact product name and conditions.
  3. Before purchasing, check price, included term, renewal conditions, any trial offer and whether the licence is tied to the vehicle or account.
  4. Follow the manufacturer’s connectivity and activation instructions. Some purchases activate remotely; other workshop activations have their own procedures.
The function is not offered

If the function is not offered

Reasons can include missing preparation, unsupported software, a different market or offer restrictions. Coding alone does not establish a valid licence or correct operation. If hardware must be added, the retrofit needs a separate assessment.

For a check, prepare the VIN, requested function, a photograph of its offer or message and system information. Mention any previous unit replacement or modification.
