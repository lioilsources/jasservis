Warning lights and driver messages
Engine, lubrication and cooling

EPC – petrol engine management

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The petrol engine management system has detected a fault. EPC does not identify a particular failed component.

Recommended action

Arrange engine inspection promptly. Brief illumination with the ignition on is a check; the lamp should go out after starting.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Emissions system – MIL steadily lit · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Emissions system – MIL steadily lit

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

An emissions system fault has been detected. The symbol alone does not identify a failed component.

Recommended action

Reduce load and arrange inspection as directed by the manual and message. If running is abnormal, other warnings appear or STOP is displayed, stop safely and seek assistance.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Flashing MIL – misfiring

Yellow / amber · Warning · Flashing

Meaning

Flashing may indicate misfiring that can damage the catalytic converter. This differs from a steadily lit MIL.

Recommended action

Reduce speed and load and arrange inspection in accordance with the message. If the engine shakes severely or loses power, stop safely and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Low engine oil pressure

Red · Warning · Steady / flashing

Meaning

Lubrication pressure is too low. A correct oil level does not confirm sufficient pressure.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. If the warning persists, even with the correct level, do not leave it idling; obtain professional assistance. Follow the manual for further steps.

Key indication

This is an example of an indication. Check its meaning for your specific model and equipment against the exact message and your vehicle’s owner’s manual.

Oil pressure – red flashing (Toledo)

Red · Warning · Flashing

Meaning

The red flashing oil can warns of insufficient oil pressure. Do not confuse it with the yellow level warning on this version.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. If the warning persists, even with the correct level, do not leave it idling; obtain professional assistance. Follow the manual for further steps.

Key indication

Only SEAT Toledo, model year 2013, in the cited manual edition. This version and meaning are not transferred to other models.

Oil level – yellow steady light (Toledo) · multiple meanings of the same symbol

Oil level – yellow steady light (Toledo)

Yellow / amber · Warning · Steady

Meaning

The steadily lit yellow oil can on this version indicates an insufficient engine oil level. It is not the red lubrication pressure warning.

Recommended action

Stop safely, switch off the engine and check the level as instructed. If topping up is necessary, use the correct oil and do not exceed the maximum level.

Key indication

Only SEAT Toledo, model year 2013, in the cited manual edition. This version and meaning are not transferred to other models.

Oil level sensor – yellow flashing (Toledo)

Yellow / amber · Warning · Flashing

Meaning

Yellow flashing with “Oil sensor. Workshop!” indicates an oil level sensor fault. The lamp does not establish that the oil level is correct.

Recommended action

Obtain professional assistance and have the sensor and fault cause checked. Follow any additional vehicle messages.

Key indication

Only SEAT Toledo, model year 2013, in the cited manual edition. This version and meaning are not transferred to other models.

Engine not yet warm – blue indicator (Toledo)

Blue · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

The engine has not yet reached its operating temperature. This is an operating indication, not the red overheating warning.

Recommended action

Avoid high engine speeds, hard acceleration and high engine loads. According to the manual, this blue version does not apply to vehicles equipped with the information display.

Key indication

Only SEAT Toledo, model year 2013, in the cited manual edition. This version and meaning are not transferred to other models.

Engine cooling – red warning (Toledo)

Red · Warning · Steady / flashing

Meaning

Red illumination or flashing indicates excessive coolant temperature or low coolant level in the combustion engine.

Recommended action

Stop safely and switch off the engine. Check the system only after safe cooling, as instructed; do not open a hot expansion tank. If the warning persists, do not continue and obtain professional assistance.

Key indication

Only SEAT Toledo, model year 2013, in the cited manual edition. This version and meaning are not transferred to other models.

Auto Hold – vehicle held, green P (Tarraco)

Green · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

On the specified Tarraco, green P lights when Auto Hold holds the vehicle. AUTO HOLD text on the physical button labels the control, not this status symbol.

Recommended action

Remain ready to brake. Secure the vehicle properly before leaving; do not leave it running relying only on Auto Hold. Disable before a conveyor car wash as instructed.

Key indication

This record documents conventional-drive equipment in the specified edition. Requirements include closed driver door, fastened belt and running engine. Not red parking-brake P or the AUTO HOLD inscription used by other models.

Lane Assist – passive, not providing guidance

Grey · Operating information · Steady

Meaning

The grey operating indicator means Lane Assist is passive and not available for guidance. It differs from the white Control Centre shortcut and does not itself prove a camera fault.

Recommended action

The driver remains responsible for steering and keeping the lane. Observe messages, operating conditions and limits in the manual.

Key indication

Green denotes availability and yellow intervention; these documented states are not conflated with grey passive status.

Lane Assist – unavailable, symbol with exclamation mark

Yellow / amber · Function restricted · Steady

Meaning

The amber Lane Assist symbol with exclamation mark and message means the assistant is unavailable. Camera-view limitations, damage or faults may be responsible; the symbol alone does not identify a failed sensor.

Recommended action

The driver must steer. Follow the manual to inspect and clean the camera view and remove obstructions or stickers. Seek a qualified workshop if the problem persists; follow vehicle messages.

Key indication

The documented state is the steady amber car-between-lanes variant with an exclamation mark and unavailable message: Golf 5H0 11.2020, ID.4 11.2021, Terramar CS 11.25 and Tarraco DE 06.2023. Amber without an exclamation mark may mean corrective intervention, switched-on inactive operation or undetected markings in another precisely cited state; amber alone does not prove a fault.
