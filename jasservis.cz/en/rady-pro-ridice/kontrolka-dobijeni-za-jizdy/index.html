Charging warning light came on while driving. What now?

  • Charging warning light came on while driving. What now?

Back to driver advice ←

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

The battery symbol alone does not identify the cause of a fault or a safe remaining driving distance. Distinguish its appearance when switching the vehicle on from a warning that remains after starting or appears while driving.

Which vehicles this advice covers

Volkswagen Group vehicles with combustion, hybrid or electric drive. The meaning of the symbol and instructions for further operation vary with the specific version.

What to do now

Read the complete message. If the vehicle instructs you to stop or not to continue, stop safely and arrange professional assistance. Whether to switch off the drive system or leave it active depends on the specific instruction; do not investigate the fault by repeatedly switching it off and on.

If the next steps are unclear, stop safely and agree them with the roadside assistance service. An estimated distance before the battery runs flat is not a reliable basis for continuing.

A check when switching on and a warning while driving

Warning lights may briefly illuminate during function checks when the vehicle is switched on. A persistent or newly appearing warning with the engine running or the drive system active is a different situation. Follow the handbook and accompanying message, not just the appearance of the symbol.

Charging does not always mean an alternator

In a combustion vehicle, the fault may concern the alternator or the associated system. In MEB platform electric vehicles, a DC/DC converter supplies the 12V network. Some mild hybrids also link their 48V and 12V systems through a converter. The warning therefore does not, by itself, confirm a faulty battery or any specific component.

What to give the workshop

  • VIN, model, type of drive and current mileage.
  • A photograph of the complete message, the warning colour and any flashing.
  • Whether the warning appeared before starting, afterwards or while driving.
  • Other changes: restricted functions, an unusual noise or a recent repair.
