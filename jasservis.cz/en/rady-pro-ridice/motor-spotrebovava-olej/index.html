The engine is using oil

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle version. Selecting a brand alone does not identify the exact vehicle.

What affects oil loss and when to investigate a fault

Which vehicles this advice applies to

Volkswagen Group vehicles with a combustion engine. The measurement method, applicable limit and diagnosis are determined for the particular engine and its documentation.

What to do now

An oil pressure warning requires a different response

Do not confuse a red oil pressure warning with a level warning or an oil change reminder. Stop safely and switch off the engine. A correct level alone does not confirm adequate lubrication pressure. Do not leave the engine running; the next steps, including any restart, must follow the handbook for the particular vehicle. If uncertain, arrange professional assistance.

What the symptom may mean

Oil needs topping up between changes. To assess this, however, it is not enough to say how many bottles you bought. What matters is the amount actually added, the distance covered and comparable conditions when checking the level. It is also important whether the rate of loss has remained stable over time or suddenly increased.

Consumption may relate to the engine's design and use, but there may also be an external leak or a technical fault. A figure taken from another engine does not by itself determine whether your vehicle is working correctly.

Permitted consumption is not one figure for every engine

The manufacturer may specify a consumption limit for a particular engine and set of conditions. That figure must be read in the context of the applicable handbook. It is not the expected consumption of every fault-free engine or a substitute for checking for leaks.

If the rate of loss suddenly increases, it is worth having the cause assessed even if it remains within a commonly quoted figure. A repair is not determined solely by comparison with an internet discussion or another car of the same brand.

Use and the initial period of operation matter

Consumption is affected by factors including engine speed, load, ambient conditions and oil properties. When it is assessed, describe actual use: short city trips, long journeys and any significant changes from before.

For a new engine, the documentation may allow higher initial consumption. That period cannot be extended arbitrarily, nor can the same distance figure explain every instance of oil loss. For a new or repaired engine, the instructions for its particular version are decisive.

Crankcase ventilation is one possibility

Crankcase pressure control can influence oil consumption. On a particular older engine generation, this influence may also relate to driving conditions. That is not a reason to attribute every oil loss to crankcase ventilation.

The checks should distinguish crankcase ventilation, external leaks and other possible causes according to the engine design. Opening the filler cap or simply judging how the engine runs does not replace this verification. Replacing the ventilation valve is not a universal repair, and topping up alone does not establish a need to dismantle the engine.

A level change may not match the amount of oil burnt

Fuel can enter the oil under certain operating conditions. On some engines, frequent short journeys in cold weather, during which the fuel admixture does not evaporate sufficiently, may contribute to this. The composition of the oil fill can therefore also affect the observed level.

Report a rising level or a message requesting that it be reduced to the workshop. It does not confirm that the engine is not consuming oil. Simply extracting the excess will not remove any oil deterioration or the cause of the problem. Do not try to resolve it with a high-load test drive.

What to tell the workshop

For a quicker discussion, have the VIN, current mileage and a brief description of the requested work or fault ready. We confirm the appointment and scope of work by agreement.

What helps assess oil loss

Observation What to provide to the workshop
Repeated top-ups The date, odometer reading and every amount actually added, together with the oil used and its approval.
A sudden change When it began, how quickly it is developing and whether it followed a repair or a change in driving conditions.
Oil traces or smoke Where and under what circumstances you notice them. Appearance alone does not yet locate the fault.
The level rises instead Whether any oil has been added and whether the way the car is used has changed. A higher level does not necessarily mean an adequate quantity of uncontaminated oil.

How the cause is checked

How to obtain a usable result

Carry out normal level checks under the conditions stated in the handbook. The vehicle's position, engine condition and reading method matter. The same procedure does not automatically apply to a dipstick and an electronic indicator. If the reading does not change immediately after topping up, do not add more oil without checking.

If there is doubt, consumption is measured using the procedure for that engine and possible leaks are checked. The initial oil fill and test conditions must be known. Only a usable result can be compared with the applicable limit and assessed alongside the other findings.
