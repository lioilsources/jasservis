Parking heater does not work or switches off too soon

  • Home
  • Driver advice
  • Parking heater does not work or switches off too soon

Back to driver advice ←

Applicability depends on the engine, equipment and vehicle specification. Selecting a brand alone does not identify a particular vehicle.

Distinguish whether the heater receives the command at all, actually starts heating, or switches off after a short time. The activation method, exact remote-control indication and sequence of events are useful. An indicator light or a white cloud alone does not identify a faulty part.

What to do now

If there is heavy or persistent smoke, a fuel smell or unusual combustion noises, switch the heater off and do not try to start it again. Arrange a professional inspection. Do not operate or programme the heater in an enclosed, unventilated garage.

If these symptoms are absent, note how the heater responds when activated from the vehicle, the app and the remote control. Also record vehicle messages, the fuel level and the running time.

Which vehicles this advice covers

Vehicles with a fuel-fired parking or supplementary heater. Electric pre-conditioning in an electric vehicle or hybrid has different operating conditions. The controls and indications must be matched to the specific vehicle, heater and remote control.

It heats through the app, but not through the remote control

Check that the app actually starts the heater, rather than just ventilation or another pre-conditioning function. If the heater starts by one method but not another under comparable conditions, this is an important distinction when checking the control path. It does not, by itself, prove that the remote control or receiver is faulty.

The remote control’s response close to the vehicle is useful information for the workshop. Depending on the particular version, obstacles, weather or the condition of its battery may reduce its range.

Exactly what does the remote-control indicator do?

Confirmation of a radio command and a report of a heater fault are different information. Check the meaning of colours and flashing in the manual for that remote control; it cannot be applied to every system.

  • Photograph both sides of the remote control, including its model designation.
  • State which button you pressed and whether the indicator remains off, stays on or flashes.
  • Record the colour, any colour changes, the number and pattern of flashes, and the total indication time; a short video helps.

Indication example: SEAT Ateca, 11/2018 manual

This table applies to the remote control described in the English SEAT Ateca manual dated 11/2018, printed pages 183–184. It is not a common guide for all vehicles or identification of a remote control from a photograph alone.

What the indicator doesMeaning in this manual
Lights up green for approximately 2 seconds.Confirmation that the heater has been switched on with the corresponding button.
Lights up red for approximately 2 seconds.Confirmation that the heater has been switched off with the OFF button.
Flashes green for approximately 2 seconds, about 4 times per second.The switch-on command was not received.
Flashes green rapidly for approximately 2 seconds, about 10 times per second.The heater is blocked. Possible causes include an almost empty fuel tank, a very low 12V battery charge or a fault.
Flashes red for approximately 2 seconds, about 4 times per second.The switch-off command was not received.
Lights up orange for approximately 2 seconds, then green or red.The remote-control battery is almost flat; the command was received.
Lights up orange for approximately 2 seconds, then flashes green or red.The remote-control battery is almost flat; the command was not received.
Flashes orange for approximately 5 seconds.The remote-control battery is flat; the command was not received.

If the indicator does not light up at all when a button is pressed, this manual calls for the remote-control battery to be replaced. Confirmation from the remote control does not, by itself, verify that the heater subsequently operates correctly. A video of the complete indication and the remote-control designation will help the workshop; do not confuse the flash rate with the total number of flashes.

It starts, but only runs briefly

An early shutdown may be related to the power supply, permitted operating conditions or a combustion fault. For the relevant Thermo Top Evo heaters, Webasto distinguishes failure to start, flame extinction, undervoltage and overheating. The cause cannot be determined from the running time alone.

The CUPRA Formentor 11/2022 manual describes shutdown when the 12V battery charge is low, the fuel reserve warning is on or the set operating time has elapsed. On this version, supplementary heating may switch on automatically after the engine is started in low outside temperatures and switch off after a period of time. This is a different situation from interrupted pre-heating while the vehicle is parked.

White vapour or heavy smoke?

The specified Formentor manual allows for vapour beneath the vehicle in cold, humid conditions without a fault. Therefore, do not automatically regard heavy, persistent smoke as normal vapour, especially if accompanied by a fuel smell or failure to start. White colour alone does not confirm a particular fault.

If there is unusual smoke, follow the safety instructions at the start of this article. Do not try to 'burn it clear' by repeatedly starting the heater.

Run-on after switching off and information for the workshop

A prescribed run-on period may follow normal switch-off. During normal operation, do not interrupt it by disconnecting the power supply. If there is dangerous behaviour, follow the instructions for the specific equipment and arrange professional assistance.

  • VIN, vehicle model, and the heater or remote-control designation, if known.
  • The activation method, vehicle messages and a video of the remote-control indication.
  • Outside conditions, approximate fuel level and whether the vehicle's engine was running.
  • How long the heater ran before stopping, whether it actually produced heat and when smoke appeared.
