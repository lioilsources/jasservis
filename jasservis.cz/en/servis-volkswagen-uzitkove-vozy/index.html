Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles servicing

  • Home
  • Vehicle servicing
  • Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles servicing

We specialize in diagnostics, maintenance and repairs of Volkswagen commercial vehicles manufactured from 2009 onwards. We mainly service Caddy, Transporter, Caravelle, Multivan, California and Crafter models; we determine the scope of work according to the design and technical condition of the vehicle.

For diagnosis, we use the original ODIS system, expert measuring equipment and manufacturer's service information. We link the measurement results with the vehicle inspection and, based on them, propose the next repair procedure.

We plan regular maintenance according to the service plan of a specific car and its operational use. We will discuss the necessary service operations and repairs with the customer in advance, including the expected scope and price, so that they can include the service visit in their operational planning.

J&S SERVIS has been operating in Statenice – Černý Vůl since 1994. We also use our experience with Volkswagen Group technology in the maintenance and repair of commercial vehicles.

  • Maintenance according to the operational use of the car
  • Original ODIS diagnostics
  • Experience with VW Group technology since 1994

Engine diagnostics and repairs

We carry out regular service inspections, replacement of engine and transmission oils, filters and timing drives. We provide diagnostics and repairs of TDI and TSI engines, turbochargers, injection, cooling and emission systems including DPF, EGR and AdBlue.

Transmissions, chassis and electronic equipment

We also service manual and automatic transmissions, brake and chassis systems, steering, air conditioning and electronic vehicle equipment.

Original ODIS diagnostics

We use ODIS, professional measuring equipment and dedicated workshop tools. We carry out fault finding, assess measured values and perform functional tests and prescribed basic settings within the vehicle's technical capabilities.

Service and maintenance of vehicles

We carry out service and repair work according to the service procedures of the manufacturer. We determine the scope of repairs based on the technical condition of the vehicle and the results of diagnostics.

Hybrid and electric vehicles

We confirm the scope of maintenance and diagnostics in advance for the specific vehicle. High-voltage traction battery repairs and work on high-voltage components are not included in this offer.
